## LEAD MAGNET: "THE EXECUTIVE'S FIRST 90 DAYS"

To create your lead magnet PDF, include:

1. **Cover page:** Title, tagline, date
2. **Table of contents**
3. **Intro:** Why the first 90 days matter (2 pages)
4. **The 30-60-90 Framework**
   - Days 1-30: Listen, learn, build relationships
   - Days 31-60: Make early wins, establish authority
   - Days 61-90: Set strategic direction
5. **Key principles** (4-5 pages of actionable advice)
6. **Your story:** Why you created this resource (1 page)
7. **Call to action:** Subscribe to The Executive Forge
8. **Back cover:** Newsletter info

**Total length:** 15-25 pages  
**Format:** PDF with professional layout, brand colors throughout
