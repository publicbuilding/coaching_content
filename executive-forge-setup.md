# THE EXECUTIVE FORGE - SUBSTACK SETUP GUIDE

## Branding Assets

**Publication Name:** The Executive Forge  
**Tagline:** Where Leaders Are Made  
**Colors:**
- Primary Red: #D32F2F
- Black: #1A1A1A
- Gold: #D4AF37
- White: #FFFFFF

## Substack Setup Instructions

1. Go to substack.com/signup
2. Create account with email
3. Name publication: "The Executive Forge"
4. Add tagline: "Where Leaders Are Made"
5. Choose free tier
6. Set up publication branding:
   - Primary color: Red (#D32F2F)
   - Accent: Gold (#D4AF37)
   - Text on dark (Black #1A1A1A with White #FFFFFF text)
7. Add publication description (see below)
8. Set up welcome sequence emails (see email sequence file)
9. Create header/banner image: Professional executive imagery with red/gold accents

## Publication Description

"The Executive Forge is where emerging and established leaders come to sharpen their edge. We deliver actionable insights, rare perspectives, and battle-tested frameworks for navigating complex decisions—all from leaders who've been in the arena. This is your weekly dose of clarity, strategy, and the unfiltered truth about what separates good leaders from great ones."

## Color Implementation Guide

- **Header/Banner:** Black (#1A1A1A) background with gold (#D4AF37) accents and white text
- **Call-to-Action Buttons:** Red (#D32F2F)
- **Accent Elements:** Gold (#D4AF37) for highlights, borders, dividers
- **Text:** Black (#1A1A1A) on light, white (#FFFFFF) on dark
- **Links:** Red (#D32F2F)
