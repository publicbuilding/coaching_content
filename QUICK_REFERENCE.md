## THE CORE PHILOSOPHY

**"We earn money because we're exceptional. Revenue is the consequence, not the goal."**

### Week 1-6 (PRIORITY: Build Authority)
- Publish free content only
- Optimize for: Audience trust, engagement, email list growth
- Accept: AdSense only (passive revenue)
- Reject: Sponsorships, courses, affiliate links

### Week 7-12 (OPPORTUNITY: Selective Monetization)
- Sponsorships: Only aligned brands you'd recommend
- Affiliate: Only products you mentioned naturally
- Courses: Only if audience is asking
- Rule: Never let revenue > 25% of focus

### Month 18+ (FOUNDATION: Premium Positioning)
- Sponsorships: $20K-50K per video (you're selective)
- Courses: Tier 1-2 generating $50-80K/month
- Coaching: Tier 3 for premium clients
- Rule: Authority was built; now monetize it

## QUALITY GATES (BEFORE PUBLISHING)

```
ACCURACY:  ✅ Every claim cited?
PROSE:     ✅ Sounds like smart person talking?
HOOK:      ✅ Would stop someone scrolling?
INSIGHT:   ✅ Novel (not obvious)?
PRODUCTION:✅ 8+/10 quality?
METADATA:  ✅ Title + description compelling?

ALL GREEN? → PUBLISH
ANY RED?   → REWRITE OR REJECT
```

## WHAT KILLS A SCRIPT

- ❌ Unverifiable claims (even one)
- ❌ Weak hook (first 3 seconds don't grab)
- ❌ Obvious conclusion (common sense, not insight)
- ❌ Weak ending (ends with "and that's why" instead of insight)
- ❌ Poor prose (sounds written, not spoken)
- ❌ Vague visuals (editor will guess wrong)
- ❌ No citations (don't claim it if you can't cite it)

## RED FLAGS (PIVOT IMMEDIATELY)

**If shorts aren't hitting 50K views by Week 3:**
- Audit hooks → Pivot to controversy/surprise
- Test 5 shorts/day instead of 2
- Don't change core strategy; refine execution

**If long-forms underperform:**
- Extend to 18-20 minutes
- Add more B-roll + graphics
- Check: Does it teach something valuable?

**If email list isn't growing:**
- Your lead magnet isn't compelling
- CTA in videos isn't clear
- Offer should be: specific, valuable, immediate

**If subscriber growth stalls:**
- Increase shorts frequency
- Check: Is engagement high? (If yes, audience is good, just needs reach)
- Test "series" format (multi-part shorts)
