# Designer Interview Questions

## Initial Screening (Async/Email)

Use 2-3 of these in first email response to gauge fit before scheduling calls:

1. **Design Philosophy**
   - "Walk us through your design process for a Gumroad sales page. How do you approach conversion-focused design?"
   - "What's your approach to maintaining brand consistency while bringing fresh creative ideas?"

2. **Retainer Experience**
   - "Have you worked retainer roles before? What made them work (or not work)?"
   - "How do you prioritize multiple requests in a retainer environment?"

3. **Tools & Workflow**
   - "What's your preferred design software? How comfortable are you switching tools if needed?"
   - "Walk us through your typical file organization system for a retainer client."

4. **Deliverables & Timeline**
   - "How many design deliverables can you realistically handle per week while maintaining quality?"
   - "What's your typical turnaround time for: (a) a Gumroad product page? (b) YouTube thumbnail? (c) social graphic?"

5. **Collaboration**
   - "Describe your experience working with non-designers on feedback. How do you handle conflicting input?"
   - "What's the best way to communicate design changes/revisions? (Email, Slack, video calls?)"

## First Call (30-45 min)

**Purpose:** Culture fit, communication style, and deeper dive into portfolio

### 1. Warm-up & Background
- "Tell me about your design journey. How did you specialize in [their niche]?"
- "What type of projects energize you most? What do you avoid?"

### 2. Portfolio Deep-Dive
- "Walk me through 2-3 of your best projects. Why did you choose these for your portfolio?"
- "Do you have any Gumroad product page design you can share? If not, would you be open to designing one as a trial?"
- "Show me a thumbnail design you're proud of. What made it effective?"

### 3. Understanding Executive Forge & Our Needs
- "What's your experience with course/educational product design?"
- "How would you approach designing for a personal brand (executive/thought leader)?"
- "What's your take on thumbnail design best practices for YouTube?"

### 4. Retainer Workflow & Expectations
- "Walk us through how you'd organize deliverables if you had 8-12 requests per week."
- "How do you handle priority shifts or rush requests without burning out?"
- "What communication cadence works best for you? (Daily standup? Weekly check-in? Async Slack?)"

### 5. Rate & Logistics
- "Walk us through how you arrived at your retainer rate. What's included?"
- "Are you open to revisions? How many revision rounds before additional fees kick in?"
- "What's your ideal project kickoff process?"

### 6. Technical & Creative Fit
- "Describe a time a client asked for something that didn't align with design best practices. How did you handle it?"
- "What design trends are you excited about right now? Any you dislike?"
- "How do you stay current with design trends?"

### 7. Long-term Vision
- "What are you looking for in a retainer role? What would make this an ideal partnership for you?"
- "Are you interested in growing into a leadership role (art director) as we scale?"
- "What would make you want to stay with a retainer client long-term?"

### 8. Red Flags to Listen For
- Vague portfolio or hesitation to share work
- Unclear on their own process/pricing structure
- Dismissive of feedback or collaboration
- Unrealistic turnaround promises
- Not asking questions about our needs/brand
- Signs of undercharging (often indicates inexperience or sustainability issues)

### 9. Closing
- "Do you have questions for us? What would help you decide if this is the right fit?"
- "If we move forward, what's your availability to start?"

## Reference Call (If Finalist)

Before final offer, call 1-2 previous retainer clients:

1. "How long did [Designer] work with you on retainer? Why did it end (or is it ongoing)?"
2. "Describe the typical turnaround and communication. Were they reliable?"
3. "How did they handle revision requests and feedback?"
4. "Any areas where they struggled or excelled?"
5. "Would you rehire them? Why or why not?"

---

## Trial Project (Optional but Recommended)

Instead of lengthy interviews, consider a **paid trial project** ($200-500):
- 1 Gumroad product page design OR 3-5 YouTube thumbnails
- 5-7 day turnaround
- 2 rounds of revisions included
- This tells you more than any interview

**What to evaluate:**
- Quality of work matching their portfolio
- Communication & responsiveness
- How they handle feedback
- File organization & deliverables
