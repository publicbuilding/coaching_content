## II. Contract Basics: The Essential Elements
**Length:** 1 page | **Time:** 2 minutes

### Every Contract Must Have These Elements

#### 1. **Offer & Acceptance**
**What it is:** One party offers something, other party accepts.

**Why it matters:**
- Creates legal binding
- Clarifies who's obligated to what
- Prevents "we never agreed to that"

**Questions to ask:**
- [ ] Is it clear what we're offering?
- [ ] Is it clear what they're accepting?
- [ ] Is acceptance automatic or requires signature?

**Red flag:** Vague language like "as discussed" or "normal terms"

#### 2. **Consideration (Exchange of Value)**
**What it is:** Both parties give something, both receive something.

**Examples:**
- We pay $10K, they deliver software
- We provide services, they provide office space
- We license IP, they pay royalties

**Why it matters:**
- One-sided agreements aren't enforceable
- Makes clear what each person is "buying"

**Questions to ask:**
- [ ] What are we giving?
- [ ] What are we receiving?
- [ ] Are both sides getting something of value?

**Red flag:** Contract benefits only one side

#### 3. **Terms & Conditions (What, When, How)**
**What it is:** Specific details of the deal.

**Includes:**
- **What:** Scope, deliverables, specifications
- **When:** Timeline, deadlines, milestones
- **How:** Quality standards, methods, requirements
- **Price:** Amount, payment terms, schedule

**Why it matters:**
- Prevents disputes over "what was supposed to happen"
- Creates objective standards for success/failure

**Questions to ask:**
- [ ] Is scope crystal clear?
- [ ] Can both parties objectively know if it's met?
- [ ] Are timelines specific (not "soon" or "ASAP")?

**Red flag:** Vague requirements like "reasonable effort" or "best efforts"

#### 4. **Term & Termination (How Long, How to Exit)**
**What it is:** When contract starts, when it ends, how to get out early.

**Includes:**
- **Start date:** When obligations begin
- **End date:** When obligations end
- **Auto-renewal:** Does it renew automatically? (If yes, requires notice)
- **Termination clauses:** Can either party exit early? Under what conditions?
- **Consequences:** What happens if someone exits early?

**Why it matters:**
- Open-ended contracts are dangerous
- Auto-renewal can trap you in bad deals
- Need clear exit ramp if relationship deteriorates

**Questions to ask:**
- [ ] Is there a specific end date?
- [ ] Does it auto-renew? If yes, requires how much notice to cancel?
- [ ] Can either party terminate early? What's the cost?
- [ ] What happens to work in progress if someone terminates?

**Red flag:** Auto-renewing contract with 60+ day notice requirement (you forget, you're stuck another year)

#### 5. **Confidentiality/IP Ownership**
**What it is:** Who owns what, who can talk about it?

**Includes:**
- **IP ownership:** Who owns output/work product?
- **Pre-existing IP:** What if one party brings existing IP into deal?
- **Confidentiality:** What's confidential? For how long?
- **Non-compete:** Can either party do similar work with competitors?

**Why it matters:**
- Determines who can monetize the work
- Prevents accidental IP theft
- Protects sensitive business information

**Questions to ask:**
- [ ] Who owns the finished work?
- [ ] Can we reuse our processes/methodologies?
- [ ] What information must stay confidential?
- [ ] How long does confidentiality last?

**Red flag:** "We own all IP forever" when you're providing services with your methodologies

#### 6. **Liability & Risk (Who's Responsible if Something Goes Wrong)**
**What it is:** Allocation of risk and responsibility for failures.

**Includes:**
- **Warranty:** What are you promising about quality/performance?
- **Liability limits:** Maximum you're liable for
- **Indemnification:** Who covers losses from third-party claims?
- **Force majeure:** What happens if something unforeseeable happens? (pandemic, earthquake, war)

**Why it matters:**
- One liability clause can bankrupt you
- Need to cap your exposure
- Need to know what you're promising (warranty)

**Questions to ask:**
- [ ] Am I warranting something I can't guarantee?
- [ ] What's my maximum liability?
- [ ] Am I indemnifying them for their mistakes?
- [ ] Does force majeure apply (protection from unforeseeable events)?

**Red flag:** "Unlimited liability" or "Indemnify client for all losses"

## III. The 5 Clauses That Matter Most
**Length:** 1 page | **Time:** 2 minutes

### #1: Payment Terms (Clarify Money)
**Must specify:**
- Amount (exact dollar amount, not "reasonable cost")
- When due (30 days from invoice? Net 60?)
- How paid (ACH, credit card, check?)
- What triggers invoice (upon completion? monthly? upfront?)
- Late payment penalties (interest, suspension of services?)

**Real problem:** "Payment upon completion" undefined = vendor waits 60+ days for money

**Better version:** "Payment due 30 days from invoice. Invoice sent upon delivery of services."

---

### #2: Termination for Cause (How to Exit If They Fail)
**Must specify:**
- What counts as "failure to perform"
- How much notice needed
- What happens to money/work if terminated
- Cure period (time to fix before termination kicks in)

**Real problem:** Contract says we can terminate but doesn't say what happens to:
- Money already paid
- Partial work
- Ongoing commitments
- Client data

**Better version:**
"If Vendor fails to deliver services as specified, Client may terminate with 5 business days notice. Client receives refund of any prepaid fees for undelivered services. Vendor returns all client data within 5 business days."

---

### #3: Limitation of Liability (Cap Your Risk)
**Must specify:**
- Maximum liability (often "equal to fees paid" or "first 12 months fees")
- Excludes indirect damages (lost profits, lost data, etc.)
- Both parties same cap

**Real problem:** Contract says you're liable for any damages = potentially infinite exposure

**Better version:**
"Neither party liable for indirect damages (lost profits, lost revenue, lost data). Total liability capped at fees paid in past 12 months."

---

### #4: Confidentiality (Protect Secrets)
**Must specify:**
- What's confidential (client lists, pricing, processes)
- How long confidentiality lasts (5 years? Perpetual?)
- Who can see it (employees only? Contractors?)
- Exceptions (can disclose if legally required? To investors?)

**Real problem:** No confidentiality clause = vendor can tell competitors everything

**Better version:**
"All client information confidential for 5 years. Can only share with employees with need-to-know. Exception: can disclose if legally required with notice to other party."

---

### #5: Dispute Resolution (How Fights Get Resolved)
**Must specify:**
- Which state law governs (matters for interpretation)
- Jurisdiction (where can lawsuit happen)
- Arbitration vs. litigation (arbitration cheaper, faster)
- Who pays for dispute resolution

**Real problem:** No clause = default to suing in court = expensive, slow, public

**Better version:**
"Any dispute resolved through binding arbitration in [state], governed by [state] law. Each party pays own legal fees unless arbitrator determines party was unreasonable."

## IV. Red Flags: The Clauses That Signal Danger
**Length:** 0.8 pages | **Time:** 1.5 minutes

| Red Flag | Danger Level | How to Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Auto-renewing with 60+ day notice | **High** | Shorten notice or remove auto-renewal |
| Unlimited liability | **Critical** | Cap at 12 months fees |
| "Reasonable best efforts" | **Medium** | Replace with specific metrics |
| No payment terms specified | **High** | Add: "Payment 30 days from invoice" |
| You indemnify them for all losses | **Critical** | Limit to losses caused by your negligence |
| Exclusive relationship/non-compete | **High** | Negotiate carve-outs (your prior work, your own brand) |
| IP = "all work belongs to client" | **High** | Unless you're developing custom work, carve out your tools/processes |
| No termination clause | **Critical** | Add termination for cause + convenience |
| Force majeure not included | **Medium** | Add (especially post-COVID) |
| No confidentiality protections | **High** | Add specific confidentiality clause |
| "All terms as discussed" | **Critical** | Everything must be in writing, specific |
| Early termination has huge penalties | **High** | Cap exit costs or add termination for cause |

## V. Standard Contracts: Template vs. Custom
**Length:** 0.5 pages | **Time:** 1 minute

### Using a Template (Good for...)
- Simple deals (one-time service, clear scope)
- Low dollar amounts ($5K-$25K)
- Vendor-provided agreements (it's their template)

**Pros:**
- Fast
- Inexpensive
- Clearly understood by both parties

**Cons:**
- May not fit your specific situation
- Can miss important protections

### Getting Lawyer to Draft (Good for...)
- Complex deals (equity, equity, partnership)
- High dollar amounts ($250K+)
- Long-term relationships
- Unusual terms

**Pros:**
- Customized to your situation
- Comprehensive protections
- Professional review before signing

**Cons:**
- Expensive ($1K-$5K)
- Takes longer
- Overkill for simple deals

### Middle Ground (Usually Best)
- Start with template
- Lawyer reviews and suggests changes
- Much cheaper than full custom draft
- Much better than no review

## VI. Negotiation Strategy: Which Clauses Matter
**Length:** 0.5 pages | **Time:** 1 minute

### Don't Negotiate (Accept These As-Is)
- Boilerplate legalese that doesn't affect you
- Industry-standard terms
- Mutual obligations equally fair to both sides

### Must Negotiate (Never Accept Default)
- Payment terms
- Liability caps
- Termination clauses
- Confidentiality
- IP ownership

### Good to Negotiate (But Reasonable Defaults Exist)
- Term length
- Renewal terms
- Specific deliverables
- Timeline
- Dispute resolution

### Prioritization Strategy
1. **Must have:** Payment terms, termination clause, liability cap
2. **Really want:** Confidentiality, IP clarity, force majeure
3. **Nice to have:** Specific performance metrics, renewal terms

## VII. Before You Sign: The Checklist
**Length:** 0.4 pages | **Time:** 0.5 minute

### Pre-Sign Checklist
- [ ] Parties clearly identified (legal names, addresses)
- [ ] Payment terms: amount, when, how
- [ ] Scope: what exactly are we agreeing to?
- [ ] Timeline: when does it start, end, milestones?
- [ ] Termination: how do we exit? At what cost?
- [ ] Liability: capped at reasonable amount
- [ ] Confidentiality: protects our information
- [ ] IP: ownership is clear
- [ ] Everything verbally discussed is in the contract
- [ ] You'd accept if this contract were enforced exactly as written
- [ ] No surprises or "gotchas" you missed

**If any answer is "no," don't sign yet.**
