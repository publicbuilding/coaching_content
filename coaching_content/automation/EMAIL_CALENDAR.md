
## SEND TIME OPTIMIZATION

### Current Recommendations

```
WELCOME SEQUENCE
├─ Email 1: Immediate (< 5 minutes)
├─ Email 2: Day 1, 9:00 AM (morning)
├─ Email 3: Day 2, 10:00 AM (late morning)
├─ Email 4: Day 3, 2:00 PM (afternoon slump)
└─ Email 5: Day 5, 11:00 AM (mid-morning)

POST-PURCHASE SEQUENCE
├─ Email 1: Immediate (transactional urgency)
├─ Email 2: Day 1, 10:00 AM (morning momentum)
├─ Email 3: Day 3, 1:00 PM (lunch time reading)
├─ Email 4: Day 7, 11:00 AM (mid-week check)
└─ Email 5: Day 14, 3:00 PM (afternoon browsing)

WEEKLY NURTURE
└─ Tuesday, 8:00 AM (before YouTube drops, fresh week)

PROMOTIONS
├─ First email: 10:00 AM (morning engagement)
├─ Mid-campaign: 2:00 PM (afternoon second wind)
└─ Last chance: 9:00-11:00 AM (early action)
```

### How to test:

1. A/B test time: 9 AM vs 2 PM for same email
2. Track opens + clicks for each variant
3. Let run for 4-5 campaigns
4. Adjust based on winner
