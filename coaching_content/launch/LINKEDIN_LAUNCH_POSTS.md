```

Point 1
Explanation

Point 2
Explanation

Point 3
Explanation
```

This looks better on mobile and gets more engagement.

- 💡 Ideas

## 🚨 WHAT NOT TO DO ON LINKEDIN

❌ **Don't be overly sales-y.** "Buy now!" performs worse than "here's what I learned."

❌ **Don't ignore comments.** Comments = credibility + visibility.

❌ **Don't post too often.** Once per day is max. 3-4x/week is ideal.

❌ **Don't be generic.** "Excited to launch!" doesn't work. Show your work.

❌ **Don't spam tags.** Tagging 10 people looks desperate.

✅ **DO share real stories.** Vulnerability builds trust.

✅ **DO teach something.** LinkedIn users want to learn.

✅ **DO ask questions.** It drives engagement and helps you understand your audience.

✅ **DO engage back.** Reply to comments within 24 hours.
