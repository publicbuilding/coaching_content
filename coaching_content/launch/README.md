**Objective:** Sustain momentum and optimize

## 📞 Support During Launch

### Issue: Low sales
**Check:**
- Are landing pages converting? (>2%)
- Is email reaching people? (>30% open rate)
- Is social media generating traffic?
- Are platforms configured correctly?

### Issue: Customer questions
**Reference:**
- WEEK1_LAUNCH_CHECKLIST.md for response times
- FAQ section in products
- Email support templates
- Customer testimonials

### Issue: Platform technical problems
**Escalate to:**
- Gumroad support (gumroad.com/support)
- Substack support (substack.com/support)
- Platform documentation

### Issue: Team overwhelmed
**Solutions:**
- Automate where possible (email sequences)
- Delegate tasks (checklist in WEEK1_LAUNCH_CHECKLIST.md)
- Prioritize customer acquisition first
- Defer nice-to-haves
