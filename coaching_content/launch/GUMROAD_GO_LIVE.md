## 💡 Pro Tips

### Pricing Strategy
- **Don't underprice.** You're not a budget seller; you're an executive resource.
- **Price for confidence, not traffic.** Better to sell 5 at $69 than 50 at $9.
- **Use price anchoring:** Show $69 (anchor) next to $49 (good option) next to $29 (impulse buy).

### Sales Copy Principles
- **Lead with the benefit, not the feature.** Not "25-page PDF" but "saves you 10 hours of trial-and-error onboarding."
- **Be specific about who it's for.** "For directors scaling their first team" beats "for managers."
- **Include one good example.** Concrete > abstract.
- **Address the "skip it" objection.** "Not for you if you've been in role 5+ years" makes it MORE appealing to right person.

### Common Mistakes to Avoid
- ❌ Writing too much. Shorter is better. Busy execs don't read walls of text.
- ❌ Using jargon. Be clear. Clarity beats cleverness.
- ❌ Promising too much. "This will transform your leadership" → "This gives you a 90-day roadmap" (specific, believable).
- ❌ Not launching because it's "not perfect." Launch with what you have.
