## PART 1: CAC BY CHANNEL

### Overview

Customer Acquisition Cost measures how much you spend to acquire one paying customer.

**Formula:**
```
CAC = Total Marketing & Sales Spend / Number of New Customers Acquired
```

**Example:**
```
Marketing Spend:        $50,000
Sales Team Cost:        $30,000
Total Cost:             $80,000
New Customers:          50
CAC:                    $80,000 ÷ 50 = $1,600 per customer
```
