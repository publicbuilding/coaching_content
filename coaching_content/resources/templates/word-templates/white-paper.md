| **Technology Readiness** | [Low/Medium/High] | Are systems capable? |
| **Budget Availability** | [Low/Medium/High] | Are resources allocated? |
| **Cultural Maturity** | [Low/Medium/High] | Is organization ready for change? |

### Phased Implementation Approach

**Phase 1: Assess & Plan (Months 1-2)**
- Activities: Current state assessment, stakeholder interviews, roadmap development
- Deliverables: Strategic assessment report, implementation roadmap
- Success Metrics: Stakeholder alignment, clear priorities

**Phase 2: Build Foundation (Months 3-4)**
- Activities: Infrastructure setup, team training, pilot program design
- Deliverables: Foundation systems in place, trained team, pilot plan
- Success Metrics: Team readiness, system performance, pilot engagement

**Phase 3: Deploy & Optimize (Months 5-8)**
- Activities: Full rollout, ongoing optimization, performance monitoring
- Deliverables: Full implementation, monitoring dashboard, optimization recommendations
- Success Metrics: Adoption rates, performance improvements, ROI tracking

**Phase 4: Scale & Sustain (Months 9+)**
- Activities: Continuous improvement, capability building, expansion planning
- Deliverables: Refined processes, advanced capabilities, scaling plan
- Success Metrics: Sustained performance, continuous improvement, strategic expansion
