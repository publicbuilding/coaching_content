# Partnership Strategy: Executive Forge

## Overview
Partnerships unlock new audiences and add credibility. Focus on non-competitive alignments that serve your audience better.

---

## PARTNERSHIP MATRIX

### Tier 1: Revenue-Generating Partnerships

#### 1. EXECUTIVE COACHING PLATFORMS
**Potential Partners:** BetterUp, Coaching Culture, The Coaching Institute

**Model:**
- Co-branded course bundle (your course + their coaching)
- Commission on referrals (5-20% depending on structure)
- White-label option: Platform could host your course

**Why This Works:**
- Complementary services (education + coaching)
- Both serve executive audience
- Natural referral loop (course → coaching upgrade)

**Revenue Potential:**
- 5-10 referrals/month × $5,000 avg coaching = $2,500-5,000/mo
- Bundled course upsell (20% commission): $500-2,000/mo

**How to Approach:**
1. Research their partner programs (usually on website)
2. Identify decision-maker (partnerships@company or CEO)
3. Email template: "Executive education + coaching partnership"
4. Propose: Course as lead magnet for coaching, mutual promotion
5. Start small (pilot partnership with 1 cohort)

**Timeline:** 6-8 weeks (from contact to launch)

## PARTNERSHIP MECHANICS

### Revenue Models

#### Model A: Commission/Revenue Share
- You create and deliver course
- Partner promotes to their audience
- You pay them 20-40% of sales

**When:** Best for large audience partners (1M+)

#### Model B: Licensing/White-Label
- You create course once
- Partner white-labels and sells to their audience
- You receive per-student fee or flat annual fee

**When:** Best for enterprise partners, corporate platforms

**Example:** 
- Per-student: $50 per course completion
- Annual: $100,000/year for unlimited licensing

#### Model C: Exclusive Bundling
- Course bundled with partner's product/service
- Discount offered to both audiences
- Revenue split (50/50 or 60/40)

**When:** Best for complementary services (coaching + education)

#### Model D: Affiliate
- Partner gets unique promo code/link
- You pay them per sale (% or flat fee)
- Tracked through their dashboard

**When:** Easy to implement, low commitment

### Partnership Agreement Template

```
PARTNERSHIP AGREEMENT: [Your Name] x [Partner Name]

1. TERM
   - Duration: 12 months (auto-renew unless cancelled)
   - Review date: [Date]

2. REVENUE SHARE
   - [Partner] receives: [20-40%] of all course sales
   - Payment: Monthly via [Stripe/ACH]
   - Minimum threshold: $100/month

3. PROMOTION
   - [Partner] commits to:
     - Email announcement: [# times/month]
     - Social media promotion: [# posts/month]
     - Website feature: [homepage/email list]
   
4. EXCLUSIVITY
   - Non-exclusive (you can partner with other [category])
   - Or exclusive (only one coaching platform, etc.)

5. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY
   - Your course content: You retain all rights
   - Partner co-branded materials: Shared ownership

6. TERMINATION
   - 30 days notice required
   - Final payout: 45 days after term end

7. DISPUTES
   - Mediation before legal action
```
