## 💡 Strategic Insights

### Market Size
- Executive coaching market: Growing 8-10% annually
- Online course market: $15B+ annually
- Executive education: Premium pricing ($500-$5000 per course)
- Our positioning: High-value B2B/B2C hybrid

### Competitive Advantage
1. **Founder Credibility:** Direct from operating executive
2. **Content Depth:** 22 comprehensive modules (not quick courses)
3. **Practical Focus:** Real examples from operating companies
4. **Community:** Leverage existing audience
5. **Multi-platform:** Available on multiple channels

### Pricing Strategy
- **Individual Module:** $199-$299
- **Full Bundle:** $1,999-$2,499
- **Group Licensing:** $10,000-$50,000+ (B2B)
- **Pricing Ladder:** Entry (module) → Mid (bundle) → Premium (groups)

### Marketing Channels
1. **Email Marketing:** Existing subscriber base
2. **Social Media:** LinkedIn, Twitter, YouTube content
3. **Content Marketing:** YouTube videos, blog posts, Substack
4. **Affiliate Marketing:** Partner networks
5. **Corporate Sales:** Direct B2B outreach
6. **PR:** Media mentions, podcast interviews
