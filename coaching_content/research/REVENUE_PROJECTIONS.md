## SENSITIVITY ANALYSIS

### What Breaks the Model?

#### Maven Cohorts
- **If underperforms by 50%:** Reduces Year 1 revenue by ~$80k-200k
- **If overperforms by 50%:** Increases Year 1 revenue by ~$80k-200k
- **Mitigation:** Start with self-paced courses as fallback

#### Corporate Partnerships
- **If 1 major deal closes:** +$100k-500k immediate impact
- **If 2+ deals close:** +$500k-1M impact (transforms model)
- **Critical:** Essential for $500k+ annual revenue

#### Coaching
- **If 10 clients close at $10k/mo:** +$100k/month (easiest high-margin lever)
- **If 0 clients:** Moderate impact (~$50k loss in Year 2)
- **Upside:** Highest margin revenue stream (90%+)

#### YouTube Growth
- **If channel reaches 100k:** Amplifies all other channels 2-3x
- **If channel stagnates:** Slows affiliate and course growth
- **Impact:** 20-40% of revenue by Year 2

### Highest Impact Levers (in order)
1. **Corporate Partnerships** (each = +$500k-2M)
2. **Coaching clients** (each = +$5-10k/month)
3. **Maven cohort scale** (3→5 per month = +$40k/month)
4. **Community size** (100→200 members = +$3-5k/month)
5. **Course sales velocity** (200→400/month = +$10k/month)
6. **Affiliate optimization** (5→15 programs = +$1-2k/month)

### Effort vs. Impact Matrix
```
HIGH IMPACT, LOW EFFORT:
✓ Affiliate programs (once set up)
✓ Sponsorships (relationships)

HIGH IMPACT, HIGH EFFORT:
✓ Corporate partnerships (sales cycle: 8-12 weeks)
✓ Teaching cohorts (5-10 hrs/week per cohort)
✓ Coaching clients (20 hrs/week per client, but $10k/month)

LOW IMPACT, LOW EFFORT:
✓ YouTube AdSense
✓ Gumroad

AVOID:
✗ Low-impact, high-effort channels
```

## SCENARIO SELECTION FRAMEWORK

### Choose CONSERVATIVE if...
- Risk-averse (prefer slow, steady growth)
- Limited time (part-time focus)
- Want to validate each stream before scaling
- Don't want to hire a team
- **Target:** 6-12 months proof of concept, then scale

### Choose MODERATE if...
- Good work ethic, willing to hustle 30-40 hrs/week
- Confident in audience and product-market fit
- Open to hiring 1-2 VAs by month 6
- Want meaningful income within 12 months
- **Target:** $100k-300k Year 1, $1-3M Year 2

- Confident you can build a $10M+ business

- Ready to build a team (3-5 people)

- **Target:** $1M+ Year 1, $5M+ Year 2, IPO/acquisition potential

## FINAL THOUGHTS

### The Upside is Real
Moving from $16k/year → $1M+/year is absolutely achievable with these platforms and execution. The model is not dependent on viral growth or luck—it's grounded in:
- Proven SaaS platforms (Maven, Circle, Teachable)
- Recurring revenue models (community, coaching)
- High-margin product (digital education)
- Existing audience (YouTube channel)

### The Secret Sauce
The jump from Moderate to Aggressive isn't just "work harder"—it's:
1. **Partnerships** (2-3 corporate deals = $1M added revenue)
2. **Premium pricing** (coaching at $10k/month vs. courses at $300)
3. **Leverage** (hiring to scale what works)
4. **Positioning** (expert brand, thought leadership)

### Next Steps
1. **Pick your scenario** (recommend Moderate)
2. **Execute Phase 1** (Months 1-3) with 100% focus
3. **Review & adjust** at Month 3 (before scaling)
4. **Commit to partnerships** starting Month 6-7 (time-intensive but high ROI)
