**You hit $5M revenue and suddenly everything is broken.**

Communication got slower. Decisions take forever. People are confused about priorities. You're working 70-hour weeks and your best people are burning out.

Here's what happened: You scaled linearly when you needed to scale exponentially. Your processes—or lack thereof—broke.

Most operators think systems *slow you down*. But the truth is the opposite: **Systems are what let you scale. Without them, you get slower, not faster.**

This module teaches you to see your business as an interconnected whole, identify your real bottlenecks, and fix them systematically. Not heroically. Systematically.
