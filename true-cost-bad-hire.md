# The True Cost of a Bad Hire

## Target Audience
- Managers and leaders making hiring decisions
- Business owners concerned with profitability
- HR professionals justifying hiring rigor

## Document Length
- **Target:** 4-5 pages
- **Read Time:** 10 minutes

---

## I. The Problem: Beyond Salary
**Length:** 0.5 page | **Time:** 1 minute

### The Conventional Thinking
- Most leaders think: cost = salary
- Example: Hire for $60K = $60K cost

### The Reality
- True cost = salary + hidden costs
- Bad hires: 2-5x salary in total impact
- Most companies never calculate it

### Why It Matters
- Hiring decisions often lack financial rigor
- Budget for hiring like any capital investment
- One bad hire can derail team/project

---

## II. Direct Costs of a Bad Hire
**Length:** 1 page | **Time:** 2 minutes

### 1. Recruitment & Hiring Costs
- Job posting and advertising: $500-$3K
- Recruiter fees (25-30% of salary): $15K-$18K
- Internal hiring time: 50-100 hours × $50-100/hr = $2.5K-$10K
- **Subtotal:** $18K-$31K

### 2. Onboarding Costs
- HR/training staff time: 40 hours × $50/hr = $2K
- Equipment, software licenses: $1K-$5K
- Training materials and programs: $500-$2K
- **Subtotal:** $3.5K-$9K

### 3. Separation & Replacement Costs
- Exit interview, administration: $500
- Severance (if applicable): 1-3 months salary = $5K-$15K
- Unemployment insurance increase: $500-$2K
- **Subtotal:** $6K-$17.5K

### Total Direct Costs: $27.5K-$57.5K
**For a $60K salary, that's 45-95% of annual compensation**

---

## III. Indirect Costs (The Hidden Killers)
**Length:** 1 page | **Time:** 2 minutes

### 1. Productivity Loss
- Ramp-up period: 3-6 months at 50% productivity
- Lost output: $30K-$60K (depending on role)
- Manager time managing performance: 20+ hours/month
- Manager cost: $1K-$3K/month × 6+ months = $6K-$18K

### 2. Quality & Compliance Issues
- Errors, mistakes, rework: $2K-$10K
- Client complaints, churn risk: $5K-$50K (varies)
- Compliance risks (depending on role): $1K-$100K
- **Real example:** Bad accountant misses tax deadline = $25K penalty

### 3. Team Impact (Often Forgotten)
- Team morale hit: 10-20% productivity drop across team
- Team size 5 people × $60K salary × 15% drop = $4.5K/month
- Duration: 3-6 months = $13.5K-$27K
- High performers leave (turnover cascade): $100K+ per person

### 4. Management Distraction
- Performance management, meetings, documentation: 20+ hours/month
- Manager salary $100K = $10K/month cost (allocated)
- Duration: 3-6 months = $30K-$60K

### Total Indirect Costs: $50K-$260K+
**Average case: $75K-$150K** (comparable to salary itself)

---

## IV. Total Cost of a Bad Hire (The Real Number)
**Length:** 0.5 pages | **Time:** 1 minute

### Cost Calculation Formula
**Total Cost = (Salary × Years until separation) + Direct Costs + Indirect Costs**

### Example Scenarios

#### Scenario A: $60K Marketing Coordinator
- Salary × 1.5 years = $90K
- Direct costs = $40K
- Indirect costs (team impact): $60K
- **Total Cost: $190K**
- **ROI of Investing in Better Hiring: $190K saved per hire**

#### Scenario B: $150K Sales Manager
- Salary × 2 years = $300K
- Direct costs = $55K
- Indirect costs (team impact + missed revenue): $150K-$300K
- **Total Cost: $505K-$655K**
- **ROI of Investing in Better Hiring: $500K+ saved per hire**

#### Scenario C: $100K Software Engineer
- Salary × 2 years = $200K
- Direct costs = $50K
- Indirect costs (rework, security risk, team impact): $100K-$200K
- **Total Cost: $350K-$450K**
- **ROI of Investing in Better Hiring: $350K-$450K saved per hire**

### The Math
- Investing $5K more in hiring = 2% increase in hiring cost
- Reducing bad hires by 10% = $20K-$50K per year saved (for small team)
- **Better hiring pays for itself immediately**

## V. The Business Impact Beyond Numbers
**Length:** 0.5 pages | **Time:** 1 minute

### Why Bad Hires Cost More Than Math
1. **Customer relationships:** Bad hire damages client trust
2. **Strategic delays:** Project delays while managing bad hire
3. **Risk exposure:** Legal, compliance, security risks
4. **Talent loss:** Good people leave toxic situations
5. **Momentum loss:** Team focus on managing, not growing

### The Ripple Effect
- One bad hire in customer-facing role: impacts customer lifetime value
- One bad hire in engineering: impacts product quality for months
- One bad hire in leadership: cascades across entire department
