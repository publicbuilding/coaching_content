## PLATFORM-SPECIFIC POSTING STRATEGY

### YOUTUBE (Primary Authority Platform)

**Posting Schedule:**
- Shorts: 3-4 daily (spread across 8 AM, 2 PM, 8 PM UTC)
- Long-form: 1 per week (Thursday, 10 AM)
- Community: 2-3 posts weekly (polls, behind-the-scenes)

**Optimization:**
- Titles follow pattern: "Why [X] [Verb] (And [Implication])"
- Thumbnails: Consistent branding, high contrast, emotional cue
- Descriptions: 200+ words with timestamps, links, CTAs
- Tags: Mix of high-volume + niche tags

**CTAs:**
- Shorts: "Subscribe for daily breakdowns. New video every week."
- Long-form: "Save this. Watch again. Share with someone who needs it."

### TIKTOK (Viral Growth Platform)

**Posting Schedule:**
- 4-5 videos daily (spread across 9 AM, 12 PM, 3 PM, 6 PM, 9 PM UTC)
- Post at least 1 trending-audio video daily (algorithm boost)

**Optimization:**
- Hook in first second (POV frame, controversy, surprise)
- Trending audio (even if repurposed to new content)
- Comment-baiting questions (forces engagement)
- Text overlays: Large, scannable, 2-3 words max per line

**Hashtags:**
- 15-20 hashtags per video (mix of high-volume + niche)
- Create branded hashtag: #ExecutiveForgeBreakdown
- Research trending hashtags weekly

**Engagement:**
- Reply to top 10 comments daily (boosts algorithm)
- Create 2-3 "duets" per week (response to comments)
- Host weekly Q&A (comment then video reply)

### INSTAGRAM (Community Building Platform)

**Posting Schedule:**
- Reels: 2-3 daily (9 AM, 3 PM, 9 PM UTC)
- Feed carousel: 2x weekly (infographics, quotes)
- Stories: 3-4 daily (behind-the-scenes, polls)

**Optimization:**
- Reels: Include trending audio + caption-heavy
- Captions: 500+ characters (storytelling, personality)
- Hashtags: 25-30 per post (mix of high/medium/niche volume)
- Engagement: Like + comment on 50+ similar accounts daily

**Community Building:**
- Respond to comments within 1 hour (boost algorithm)
- Feature user comments in Stories
- Create "Save" moments (content people bookmark)

### LINKEDIN (Professional Authority)

**Posting Schedule:**
- Videos: 1-2 per week (Thursday, Sunday)
- Thought leadership posts: 2-3 per week (Monday, Wednesday, Friday)
- Engagement: Daily (comment on peers' posts)

**Optimization:**
- Titles: Professional, benefit-driven ("How to..." "Why..." "The Truth About...")
- First line: Hook (commands attention, invites scroll)
- Tone: Professional but accessible, no jargon
- CTAs: Thought-provoking questions, not "click here"

**Hashtags:**
- 10-15 hashtags (professional relevance)
- Mix: #BusinessStrategy #Innovation #Leadership #Entrepreneurship

**Engagement:**
- Daily: Comment meaningfully on 10-15 posts (build network)
- Weekly: Share others' content (build goodwill)
- Network: Connect with 20 relevant professionals daily
