## LINKEDIN-SPECIFIC OPTIMIZATION

**Tone Strategy:**
- Professional but accessible
- Educational (not lecture-y)
- Authority-building (cite frameworks like Christensen)
- Conversation-starting (end with thoughtful question)

**Visual Approach:**
- Clean, professional design (aligned with LinkedIn brand)
- Minimal animation (subtlety over flash)
- Data visualization (charts, comparisons)
- Professional color palette

**Caption Strategy:**
"Why did Blockbuster collapse while Netflix thrived?

Most people point to 'technology.' But the real reason is more strategic.

Blockbuster's business model required customer pain (late fees) to be profitable. Netflix eliminated that pain entirely.

This reveals a critical principle: Your competitive advantage often lies where competitors depend on friction.

Christensen called this the 'Innovator's Dilemma'—incumbents cannot disrupt their own profitable models.

So ask yourself:
→ What friction exists in your industry?
→ Do your competitors depend on it?
→ Can you remove it without destroying your model?

If you can answer yes, you've found the disruption opportunity.

Comment: What industry is due for this kind of disruption?"
