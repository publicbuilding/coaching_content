KEY FINDINGS SUMMARY:
================================================================================

FIVERR PRICING (For Full Custom Asset Pack):
  Conservative Budget: $1,450-$2,300
  - Character sprites (3-4 NPCs): $400-600
  - Buildings/structures: $350-500
  - Tileset creation: $300-500
  - UI package: $250-400
  - Misc effects: $150-300

  Premium Quality: $3,500-$5,100

FREE/OPEN ASSET PLATFORMS:
  ✅ Kenney.nl - GOLD STANDARD (100% free, CC0 license)
     • 2,000+ free assets
     • Isometric buildings, characters, UI
     • Professional quality, consistent style
     • Perfect for 60-70% of game needs

  ✅ OpenGameArt.org - Community resource (100% free, CC-licensed)
     • Thousands of free assets
     • Good for specific needs (buildings, characters)
     • Quality varies, but generally decent

  ✅ itch.io - Mixed marketplace (free + low-cost packs)
     • Many free packs available
     • Good for specialized themes
     • Requires careful filtering

ASSET CREATION TOOLS:
  ✅ Piskel - Free browser-based editor
     • No installation needed
     • Perfect for quick edits and learning
     • Professional output quality
     
  ✅ Aseprite - Professional pixel art tool ($20)
     • Industry standard
     • Advanced animation features
     • Worth it if creating many sprites
     
  ❌ AI Art Tools - Emerging, not yet reliable
     • Use for concept inspiration only
     • Not suitable as primary asset source

PHASE 4: POLISH & ITERATION (Ongoing, $0-500)
  1. Integrate all assets cohesively

  3. Iterate based on playtesting feedback
  4. Commission only essential missing pieces

CRITICAL SUCCESS FACTORS:
================================================================================

✅ Do This:
  • Start with FREE assets first (no reason to pay immediately)
  • Verify Fiverr artist portfolio BEFORE commissioning
  • Create detailed brief with reference images for artist
  • Test asset consistency in game engine early
  • Document all licenses (CC0 vs CC-BY vs OGA-BY)
  • Maintain asset inventory spreadsheet
  • Get commercial use license explicitly confirmed

❌ Avoid This:
  • Don't mix different pixel resolutions (16x16 with 64x64)
  • Don't ignore isometric perspective consistency
  • Don't assume Fiverr artists understand game needs
  • Don't commission without testing free alternatives first
  • Don't use AI art as primary asset source (unreliable)
  • Don't forget to check licenses (can cause legal issues)
