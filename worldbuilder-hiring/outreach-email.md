## Follow-Up Sequence (If Interested)

**Email 1:** Initial outreach (above) ← Wait 3-5 days

**Email 2:** Send asset-brief.md + request rate quote
- "Attached is the full asset brief. Does this scope match your interests?"
- "What's your typical rate for a package like this?"
- "Are you available to start in [timeline]?"

**Email 3:** Schedule discovery call (if they express interest)
- "Great! Let's chat about this. Are you free for a 30-min call [specific times]?"
- Goal: Discuss style alignment, revision process, communication preferences

**Email 4:** Send rate proposal + contract discussion
- Finalize scope, budget, delivery schedule
- Discuss revision rounds, communication style, IP/usage rights
- Set kickoff date

---

## Key Points to Emphasize

✓ **Creative Freedom:** "We want your artistic vision, with direction where it matters"
✓ **Ongoing Opportunity:** "This is the beginning—successful launch means more work"
✓ **Clear Briefs:** "You'll have detailed specs, but also flexibility to iterate"
✓ **Responsive Feedback:** "We'll review work quickly so you're not blocked"
✓ **Real Game:** "This is shipping—your work will be seen by players"

---

## Red Flags to Watch

🚩 Artist ghosts after initial interest (communication issues)
🚩 Quotes 2-3x your budget without discussion
🚩 Seems more interested in theory than practical delivery
🚩 Portfolio shows inconsistent shipping history
🚩 Unavailable for next 2+ months (timing risk)

---

## Success Metrics

✅ Response rate: Aim for 50%+ (3/5 candidates reply)
✅ Interest rate: Aim for 60%+ of replies (at least 1-2 seriously interested)
✅ Timeline: Find final artist within 2-3 weeks of first outreach
✅ Rate negotiation: Land within 10-20% of budget estimate
