## ❓ FAQ

### "Should I contact all 5 artists?"
**No.** Contact your top 3:
1. Somi Kim (preferred, Asian aesthetic match)
2. Evlyn Moreau (infrastructure specialist)
3. Kenney or Lottie Bevan (backup options)

### "What if they're too expensive?"
**Negotiate:**
- Extended timeline (8-10 weeks) → lower day rate
- Reduced scope (cut animations) → lower cost
- Retainer deal (ongoing work) → better rate
- Phased delivery (pay per milestone) → easier budgeting

### "How long should I wait for responses?"
- **Initial:** 3-5 days (reasonable for busy freelancers)
- **Follow-up:** Send second email at day 5-7 if no reply
- **Decision:** Pick your artists by day 14

### "Do I need all 5 assets categories?"
**For MVP:** Priority order:
1. **Characters** (NPCs drive engagement)
2. **Buildings** (core gameplay visual)
3. **UI elements** (usability critical)
4. **Terrain** (sets atmosphere)
5. **Animations** (polish/juice, can defer to post-launch)

### "Can I start with just one artist?"
**Yes.** Phased approach:
- **Week 1-2:** Characters + 3-4 building mockups ($400-600)
- **If good:** Expand to full team
- **If not:** Pivot without sinking full budget

## 📝 Contract Essentials

When you reach agreement with an artist, include:

✅ **Scope:** Link to asset-brief.md
✅ **Timeline:** Specific delivery dates (milestones)
✅ **Payment:** Milestone-based (30% → 40% → 30%)
✅ **Revisions:** Max 5 rounds included
✅ **IP:** You own final assets; artist keeps credit
✅ **Communication:** Response time expectations
✅ **Late fees:** Optional (5-10% discount per week delay)
