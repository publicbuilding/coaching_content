## PAGE DESIGN NOTES

### Visual Hierarchy
- Hero section: 80% of viewport (big, bold, clear)
- Subheadings: 28-32px (scannable)
- Body copy: 16-18px (readable)
- Form: Centered, prominent, slightly elevated visually

### Color Palette
- Primary: Professional blue (#0052CC or similar)
- Secondary: Light gray background (#F5F5F5)
- Accent: Teal or green for buttons/highlights
- Text: Dark gray (#333333), never pure black

### Form Optimization
- Auto-focus email field on load
- Mobile-responsive (single column on mobile)
- Submit button: Large, obvious, contrasting color
- Success message: "Check your inbox" (with clear next steps)

### Trust Elements
- Professional photography (headshots for testimonials)
- Company logos of companies using this (if applicable)
- "2,000+ executives" social proof number (update as you grow)
- Security badge (if using email platform)

### Mobile Optimization
- Stacked layout on mobile
- Larger form fields
- Simpler navigation
- Video instead of image (if applicable)
