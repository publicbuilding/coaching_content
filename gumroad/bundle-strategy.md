## Pricing Psychology

### The Three-Tier Effect
By offering three purchase options (individual, starter bundle, full bundle), you:
- Make the bundle seem more valuable (comparison)
- Make the full bundle seem like obvious choice ($139.99 vs. $149.95 total value)
- Give enough autonomy that people don't feel forced
- Create "upgrade path" psychology

### The Discount Structure
- **11% on starter bundle** - Meaningful but not huge (people feel they got something)
- **7% on full bundle** - Smaller discount on more expensive item (anchors price perception)
- **Early pricing 28% off** - Creates urgency without devaluing regular price

## Upsell/Cross-Sell Opportunities

### Post-Purchase Sequence
After someone buys any module:
1. **Thank you email** - Instant access + how to use it
2. **Outcome email (Day 3)** - "Here's what people usually implement first"
3. **Social proof email (Day 5)** - Real results from others
4. **Upsell email (Day 7)** - "These 2 modules pair perfectly with what you just bought"
5. **Limited-time offer (Day 10)** - Bundle completion discount

### Specific Upsells
- **After Financial Analysis** → Systems Thinking (better operations = better finances)
- **After Team Building** → Negotiation Tactics (negotiate better salaries, equity, partnerships)
- **After Systems** → Acquisition Mastery (now scale revenue with fixed ops)
- **After Acquisition** → Negotiation Tactics (negotiate better vendor/partnership terms with volume)

## Promotional Calendar (First 90 Days)

### Phase 1: Launch (Weeks 1-4)
- **Early Founder Circle pricing** (all modules discounted)
- Focus: Build momentum, get early testimonials
- Daily/weekly content about each module
- Goal: 50 sales, strong social proof

### Phase 2: Normalization (Weeks 5-8)
- Transition to regular pricing ($29.99 individual, $139.99 full)
- Focus: Bundle positioning
- Testimonial/case study marketing
- Goal: 100+ sales, establish pricing authority

### Phase 3: Optimization (Weeks 9-12)
- A/B test bundle pricing ($129.99 vs. $139.99)
- Test seasonal/outcome-based promotions
- Build email nurture sequences
- Goal: LTV optimization, repeat/referral sales

## Key Metrics to Track

### Launch KPIs
- **Individual module sales** (indicator of what resonates)
- **Bundle attach rate** (% of purchases that are bundles vs. individual)
- **Average order value** (track as you optimize)
- **Customer acquisition cost** (via marketing)
- **Refund rate** (quality indicator)

### Success Targets (Month 1)
- 20%+ of customers buy bundles
- Average order value $80+
- <5% refund rate
- 50+ customers (social proof)
