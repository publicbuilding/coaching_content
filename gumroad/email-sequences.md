### Email 1: Value First (Week 1)
**Subject Line:** "The one number every founder should know (and most don't)"

**Body:**
Here's a question: What's your customer lifetime value?

Not a rough guess. The actual number.

Can you calculate it?

Most founders can't. They know how much revenue they make. They don't know what each customer is actually worth.

This matters because it changes every decision:
- How much you can spend to acquire a customer
- Whether it makes sense to hire someone
- What margin you need to hit
- Whether a customer is worth keeping

The formula is simple:

**LTV = (Average Customer Revenue) × (Customer Lifespan in Years) × (Profit Margin)**

Example: If your customer spends $500/year, stays 3 years, and you have 40% margin, your LTV is $600.

If you can acquire that customer for $100, you have a 6x LTV:CAC ratio. That's a good business.

If you're spending $300 to acquire them, you have a 2x ratio. That's a problem.

Most founders don't know their LTV:CAC ratio. So they're guessing about growth.

Don't be most founders.

Calculate it this week. Everything becomes clearer.
