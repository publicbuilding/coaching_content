# Platform-Specific Content Guidelines

## A. YOUTUBE (Shorts + Long-Form)

### YOUTUBE SHORTS (Under 60 seconds)

**Format Specifications:**
- Aspect ratio: 9:16 (vertical)
- Resolution: 1080×1920 (minimum)
- File format: MP4, AVI (H.264 codec preferred)
- Max file size: 100GB
- Audio: Mono or stereo

**Hook Timing (CRITICAL):**
- First 1-2 seconds: Visual hook (text overlay, shocking stat, question)
- Frame 2: Audio hook (narrator speaks compelling statement)
- Frame 3-5: Deliver promise ("Here's why...")
- Frames 6-55: Main content delivery
- Frame 55-60: CTA (Subscribe button prompt, link card)

**Title Best Practices:**
- 30-60 characters (fits mobile)
- Lead with number or question
- Use power words: "Why," "How," "Shocking," "Secret"
- Examples:
  - "Why Coca-Cola Failed in India (3 Reasons)"
  - "The Untold Truth About Amazon's Supply Chain"
  - "How Niche Brands Destroy Big Corporations"

**Text on Screen Cues:**
- Minimum: 16pt font (readable on mobile)
- Max: 3-4 lines of text per frame
- Placement: Top 20%, bottom 20% (avoid center where captions auto-appear)
- Color: High contrast (white text on dark overlay OR black on light)
- Timing: Hold text 2-3 seconds minimum

**Hashtag Strategy:**
- 3-5 hashtags (not more; diminishing returns)
- Include 1-2 trending hashtags (#shorts, #businesstok)
- Include 1-2 niche hashtags (#businessmodels, #economics)
- Example set:
  - #shorts #businesstok #casestudy #economics #entrepreneurship

**Thumbnail (if enabled for Shorts):**
- Not visible in feed, but visible in "Your Shorts" suggestions
- Recommendation: 1280×720 (16:9 aspect ratio for best representation)
- Bold colors, high contrast, minimal text

**CTA Strategy:**
- Spoken CTA: "Subscribe for more business breakdowns" (last 5 seconds)
- Visual CTA: "SUBSCRIBE NOW" text overlay (last 3 seconds)
- End card: Link to related Shorts or long-form video
- Don't use: "Like and comment" (low engagement value)

**Performance Metrics to Track:**
- Click-through rate (CTR) to channel from Shorts feed
- Average view duration (should be 50%+)
- Subscriber acquisition rate from Shorts
- Engagement signals: Likes, shares, comments

### YOUTUBE LONG-FORM (15-25 minutes)

**Format Specifications:**
- Aspect ratio: 16:9 (landscape)
- Resolution: 1920×1080 (minimum; 4K preferred for quality positioning)
- File format: MP4 (H.264 codec)
- Bitrate: 8-15 Mbps (video) + 256 kbps (audio)
- Frame rate: 24fps (cinematic) or 30fps (standard)
- Max file size: 256GB

**Title Best Practices (80 characters max):**
- Lead with benefit or intrigue
- Include keyword (algorithm index)
- Avoid clickbait (damages CTR, watch time)
- Power structure: [Hook] — [Topic] | [Angle]
- Examples:
  - "Why Tesla's Supply Chain is Unbeatable (The Math)"
  - "The Untold Story of Stripe's $100B Valuation"
  - "How Netflix Destroyed Blockbuster in 3 Moves"

**Description Template (2-3 paragraphs):**
```
[HOOK - 1-2 sentences summarizing video]
[SPONSORSHIP DISCLOSURE if applicable]

[KEY POINTS - Bullet list of 3-5 main takeaways]

[TIMESTAMPS - Chapter breaks with timecodes]
[00:00 - Intro]
[02:15 - Context]
[05:30 - Main Story]
[12:45 - Analysis]
[14:20 - Conclusion & CTA]

[RESOURCES - Links to affiliate products, courses, related videos]

[CALL TO ACTION - Patreon, Email list, Discord]

Subscribe for more: [Link]
Executive Forge Premium: [Link]
```

**Thumbnail Best Practices (1280×720):**
- Rule of thirds: Position main visual/face in upper left 1/3
- High contrast (neon colors for text)
- Minimal text: 3-5 words max
- Include number if applicable (data-driven content)
- Avoid: Pixelation, tiny text, cluttered designs
- A/B test: Create 2-3 variations; YouTube auto-tests
- Structure: [Main image] + [Text overlay] + [Number/arrow]

**Chapter Markers (Timestamps):**
- Create chapters in video editor before upload
- Improves watch time distribution (viewers jump to relevant sections)
- Max 10 chapters per video (YouTube limit)
- Format: 00:00 Chapter Name

**Mid-Roll Ads (15+ minutes only):**
- Place at natural narrative breaks (not mid-sentence)
- Recommendation: 3-5 min, 8-10 min, 12-14 min marks
- Don't force; only if content naturally supports
- Increases CPM by 30-50% but may reduce watch time if jarring

**End Card Strategy (last 20 seconds):**
- Subscribe prompt + channel icon (always)
- Related video recommendation (1-2 top performing videos)
- Playlist suggestion (creates series)
- Create end screen in YouTube Studio (no editing needed)

**CTA Best Practices:**
- Spoken CTA (14-17 min mark): "Join the Executive Forge community for deeper insights" [pause] "Subscribe now"
- Visual CTA: "SUBSCRIBE + BELL" text overlay (3 seconds, 18 min mark)
- Voiceover CTA: Soft integration, not forced
- Avoid: "Smash the like button" (low perceived value; hurts viewer experience)
- Recommended: "If this changed how you think, share with someone who needs to hear it"

**Hashtag Strategy (3-5 tags):**
- 1-2 trending: #YouTube, #businesseducation, #casestudy
- 1-2 niche: #businessmodels, #entrepreneurship, #economics
- 1 branded (future): #ExecutiveForge

**Playlist Organization:**
- Create playlist by topic (Business Models, Economics, Entrepreneurship)
- Playlists drive 20-30% of repeat viewing
- Auto-add all videos to "All Videos" master playlist

**Monetization Notes:**
- Avoid: Profanity, controversial politics, "demonetizing" topics (clickbait fear, extreme violence)
- Safe topics: Business, economics, entrepreneurship, education
- CPM higher for US/UK viewers in business content ($3.50-5.50 vs. $1-2 global)

**Performance Tracking:**
- Goal: 50%+ average view duration (measure of retention)
- Track: Click-through rate (CTR) to video from search/suggested
- Goal: 3-5% conversion to subscribers per video
- Monitor: Engagement rate (comments, likes, shares as % of views)

## B. INSTAGRAM REELS (15-90 seconds)

**Format Specifications:**
- Aspect ratio: 9:16 (vertical)
- Resolution: 1080×1920 (minimum)
- Frame rate: 24-30fps
- File format: MP4, MOV
- Max file size: 4GB
- Recommended duration: 30-60 seconds (Instagram's algorithmic sweet spot)

**Hook Timing (CRITICAL for Instagram):**
- Frame 1-3 seconds: Visual hook (text overlay, scene change, or question)
- Audio hook: Trending audio + voiceover simultaneously
- Promise fulfilled by frame 5 seconds
- Content delivery: Frames 6-50
- Ending: Frame 50-60 with CTA + call to action

**Caption Strategy (Instagram's algorithm favors captions):**
- Lead with hook (emoji + powerful statement)
- 3-5 sentences (people read on mobile)
- Include CTA: "Save this," "Share if you agree," "Link in bio"
- Hashtag placement: Separate comment (not caption) to avoid clutter
- Example caption:
```
💼 Here's why Stripe is worth $95B when competitors aren't

The difference isn't the product—it's the obsession with developer experience. 

[3-4 sentences explaining]

Save this breakdown. Link to full analysis in bio 👆
```

**Hashtag Strategy (30 max, but use 15-20 best):**
- Mix: 5 trending, 5 niche, 5 branded
- Post hashtags in first comment (algorithm prefers this over caption)
- Examples:
  - Trending: #businessreels #casestudy #entrepreneurship
  - Niche: #stripepayments #businessmodels #startupstory
  - Branded: #executiveforge #businessbreakdown

**Audio Strategy (Instagram Reels REQUIRE trending audio):**
- Use trending sounds from Instagram's library (algorithm boost)
- Layover voiceover on trending audio
- Sync audio to narrative (lip-sync not required, but cadence matters)
- Source: Instagram's "Reels" tab → Sounds → Trending

**Visual Elements:**
- B-roll: Quick cuts (2-3 seconds per clip)
- Text overlays: Key stats, questions, transitions
- Colors: Brand consistent (recommend: business blues, whites, accent colors)
- Transitions: Quick dissolves, zoom cuts, slide transitions
- Avoid: Slow pans, long holds (mobile viewers have short attention)

**Performance Metrics:**
- Goal: 3-5 seconds average watch duration (Instagram Reels)
- Reach: 50K-500K impressions (heavily algorithmically distributed)
- Engagement: 3-8% (likes + comments + shares)
- Saves: Instagram weights "saves" heavily (bookmark value)

**CTA Best Practices:**
- Spoken: "Save this for later—you'll want to reference it"
- Visual: "SAVE THIS 👆" text overlay (last 3 seconds)
- Avoid: "Follow" CTA (less effective than "Save")
- Better: "Share this with someone building a business"

**Link Strategy:**
- Instagram allows 1 link in bio (use strategically)
- Use link shortener + UTM parameters to track traffic source
- Mention in video: "Full deep-dive and resources in bio"
- Link destination: YouTube video, Executive Forge course, email signup

## C. TIKTOK (15-60 seconds)

**Format Specifications:**
- Aspect ratio: 9:16 (vertical, full screen)
- Resolution: 1080×1920 (minimum)
- Frame rate: 24-60fps (60fps preferred for smooth scrolling)
- File format: MP4, MOV, AVI
- Max file size: 287.6MB (uploaded)
- Recommended duration: 21-34 seconds (TikTok's algorithmic sweet spot)

**Hook Timing (TikTok viewers are FAST to scroll):**
- Frame 0-0.5 seconds: Visual shock (bright colors, text, movement)
- Frame 0.5-1 second: Audio hook (trending sound, unexpected statement)
- Frame 1-3 seconds: Promise ("Here's why...")
- Frames 4-45: Deliver value
- Frame 45-60: CTA (follow, share, comment to vote)

**Caption Strategy (TikTok caption = visible on video):**
- Keep it MINIMAL (3 words max visible during video)
- Use caption to amplify audio message
- Example captions:
  - "POV: You ignored this business lesson"
  - "The reason you're not rich yet"
  - "This startup's secret sauce 👇"
- Full description text: Use for context, hashtags, CTA

**Sound Strategy (TikTok's ALGORITHM REQUIRES trending sounds):**
- Start with trending sound from TikTok Sound Library
- Recommended sounds for business content:
  - "Nobody:" format (trending meme audio)
  - Trending podcast clips (Joe Rogan, Tim Ferriss clips)
  - Trending motivational sounds
- Voiceover technique: Sync speech to natural pause points in trending audio
- DO NOT use copyrighted music (TikTok will suppress reach)

**Trending Hooks (TikTok loves these formats):**
- "POV: [Situation]"
- "[Statistic] of [demographic] don't know..."
- "[Celebrity/Brand] failed because..."
- "The secret reason [success story]..."
- "Why [company] makes more than..."

**Visual Trends to Leverage:**
- Jump cuts (2-3 second clips)
- Text-based transitions (word fades to next scene)
- Green screen (real footage + text overlay)
- Trend participation (TikTok challenges for business/education hashtag)
- B-roll: Stock footage, minimal branding

**Hashtag Strategy (use 3-5, not more):**
- 1-2 trending: #FYP, #ForYouPage, #Viral, #BusinessTok
- 1 niche: #BusinessModels, #Entrepreneurship, #CaseStudy
- 1 branded: #ExecutiveForge
- Avoid: Hashtag overload (looks spammy)

**CTA Strategy (different than YouTube/Instagram):**
- Spoken: "Drop a comment if you'd watch a full breakdown"
- Visual: "FOLLOW FOR MORE 👆" (text overlay, last 3 seconds)
- Engagement hook: "Who does this remind you of?" (prompts comments)
- Goal: Get 50+ comments (TikTok algorithm heavily weights engagement)

**Performance Metrics:**
- Goal: Finish rate 70%+ (measure of compelling content)
- Reach: 100K-2M impressions (TikTok distributes aggressively)
- Engagement: 5-15% (comments + shares weighted heavily)
- Video saves: Weight toward watch history relevance

**Posting Strategy:**
- Post 3-5x per week (TikTok rewards consistency)
- Optimal times: 6-10 AM, 7-11 PM (based on audience)
- Use TikTok Creator Fund analytics to identify peak times

## D. LINKEDIN (Professional, long-form + carousels)

**Format Specifications (Video Posts):**
- Aspect ratio: 1:1 (square) or 16:9 (landscape preferred)
- Resolution: 1920×1080 (minimum)
- File format: MP4, MOV
- Max file size: 10GB
- Max duration: 10 minutes (rarely exceeded)
- Recommended: 30-90 seconds

**LinkedIn Specific Rules:**
- No YouTube embeds (LinkedIn prefers native video)
- Upload directly to LinkedIn (not via link)
- Native video generates 10x more reach than linked video
- Subtitles auto-generated; verify accuracy

**Caption Strategy (LinkedIn values detailed captions):**
- Start with hook + professional credibility
- Format: [Hook] → [Why it matters] → [3 key points] → [CTA]
- Example:
```
When Stripe raised its Series C, most investors missed the real play.

It wasn't the payment processing—it was the developer obsession.

Here's what that teaches us about building $95B companies:

1️⃣ [Point 1]
2️⃣ [Point 2]
3️⃣ [Point 3]

What company do you think will be next? Drop your thoughts below 👇
```

**Tone (CRITICAL for LinkedIn):**
- Professional but conversational
- Include personal credibility statement (if applicable)
- Use first-person sparingly
- Focus: Actionable insight, thought leadership
- Avoid: Hype language, overly casual tone

**Hashtag Strategy (5-10 tags, include industry-specific):**
- Professional: #LinkedIn, #BusinessStrategy, #Entrepreneurship
- Industry-specific: #StartupStory, #ProductStrategy, #Leadership
- Branded: #ExecutiveForge, #BusinessEducation

**Video Content Format (most effective on LinkedIn):**
- Talking head (narrate over slides) - highest engagement
- Text + voiceover (animated graphics)
- Screen capture + narration
- B-roll with text overlay + voiceover

**CTA Best Practices:**
- Spoken: "Share your thoughts in the comments—what would you do?"
- Visual: Call-to-action card (LinkedIn native feature)
- Soft upsell: "For deeper dives, subscribe to [YouTube channel]"
- Better than YouTube: "Connect with me for business strategy insights"

**Series/Carousel Posts (LinkedIn native format):**
- Format: 1-5 image carousel posts (not video)
- Use case: Breaking down complex topics into steps
- Example carousel:
  - Slide 1: Hook (Why Stripe's strategy is genius)
  - Slide 2-4: Three lessons (one per slide)
  - Slide 5: CTA (Connect with me)
- Performance: Carousels generate 3x more engagement than single images

**Performance Metrics:**
- Goal: 3-5% engagement rate (comments + reactions + shares)
- Reach: 2K-20K impressions (LinkedIn has smaller, more engaged base)
- Click-through rate to resources: 1-3%
- Comment quality > quantity (LinkedIn values substantive discussion)

**Posting Strategy:**
- Post 2-3x per week (consistency important)
- Optimal times: Tuesday-Thursday, 8 AM - 5 PM (business hours)
- Avoid weekends (low professional engagement)

## E. CROSS-PLATFORM ASSET REPURPOSING STRATEGY

### From Long-Form YouTube Video → Multiple Platforms

**Step 1: Extract Clips (15-20 per 15-min video)**
- Create 60-second, 30-second, 15-second versions
- Pull high-engagement segments (usually data-heavy or surprising moments)
- Add text overlays for clip versions (will be muted on some platforms)

**Step 2: Platform Optimization**
| Platform | Clip Length | Format | Optimization |
|----------|-----------|--------|-------------|
| YouTube Shorts | 30-60 sec | 9:16 | Add captions, trending sounds |
| Instagram Reels | 30-60 sec | 9:16 | Trending audio + caption CTA |
| TikTok | 21-60 sec | 9:16 | Trending sound + engagement hook |
| LinkedIn Video | 30-90 sec | 1:1 or 16:9 | Add captions, professional tone |

**Step 3: Scheduling**
- Day 1 (Tuesday): YouTube long-form upload
- Day 1 (evening): LinkedIn post
- Day 2-4 (Wed-Fri): YouTube Shorts (1 per day)
- Day 2-4 (Wed-Fri): Instagram Reels (1 per day)
- Day 2-4 (Wed-Fri): TikTok posts (1 per day)
- Total reach: 1 hero asset generates 15+ distributed pieces

## F. THUMBNAIL & VISUAL BRAND CONSISTENCY

### Thumbnail Template (YouTube)

**Design Layers:**
1. Background: Bright, contrasting color (business blues, neon yellow, orange)
2. Main image: Relevant product/person/data visualization (rule of thirds)
3. Text overlay: 2-4 words max, bold font (Arial Black or similar)
4. Number/arrow: If applicable, highlight key data point
5. Color palette consistency: Use 2-3 brand colors across all thumbnails

**Thumbnail Testing Protocol:**
- Create 2-3 variants of each thumbnail
- YouTube allows A/B testing in Studio
- Monitor CTR; switch to best performer after 24 hours
- Keep testing data to identify patterns

### Brand Color Palette (Recommended for Executive Forge)
- Primary: Navy Blue (#001F3F) - trust, professionalism
- Secondary: Bright Orange (#FF6600) - energy, attention
- Accent: White (#FFFFFF) - clarity
- Highlight: Neon Green (#39FF14) - emphasis (data numbers)
