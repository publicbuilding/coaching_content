# Executive Forge: Quality Standards & Production Checklist

**Purpose:** Every deliverable meets publishable standards. Nothing ships that would make viewers disappointed.

---

## FIRST PRINCIPLE: "Would I Watch This Twice?"

Before any content goes into production:
- [ ] Would you personally watch this video twice?
- [ ] Would you share it with someone you respect?
- [ ] Does it teach something that compounds in value?

If answer to any is "no," refine or cut it.

---

## SCRIPT-LEVEL STANDARDS

### Accuracy - FLAWLESS
**Every claim must be:**
- [ ] Verifiable from primary source (not hearsay)
- [ ] Cited with [Author, Year]
- [ ] Contextually accurate (not misleading even if technically true)
- [ ] Sourced from 2015+ (unless foundational: Christensen 1997, Veblen 1899)

**Red flags that kill accuracy:**
- "Studies show..." without citing which study
- "Everyone knows..." (no, they don't)
- Round numbers without exact figures ($1 million instead of $987,442)
- Extrapolations without caveats ("if this trend continues..." NEEDS the caveat)

### Prose - BEAUTIFUL
**Requirements:**
- [ ] Opening sentence makes reader stop scrolling (use tension, surprise, contrast)
- [ ] Every sentence earns its space (delete if just supporting info)
- [ ] Vocabulary is specific (not vague: "Netflix dominates streaming" → "Netflix owns 40% of streaming revenue")
- [ ] Rhythm varies (short. Medium sentence. Longer sentences that build complexity.)
- [ ] Conclusion lands with insight, not platitude

**Test:** Read the script aloud. Does it sound like a smart friend explaining something? Or does it sound like you're reading a Wikipedia article?

### Structure - TIGHT

**For Shorts (50 seconds):**
- [ ] Seconds 0-2: Hook (jaw-drop moment or question)
- [ ] Seconds 2-8: Why you should care (setup)
- [ ] Seconds 8-45: Explanation (the meat)
- [ ] Seconds 45-50: Insight + CTA (save, subscribe, etc.)

**For Long-Form (15 minutes):**
- [ ] Minutes 0-1.5: Hook + premise
- [ ] Minutes 1.5-4: Context (why people got this wrong)
- [ ] Minutes 4-11: Deep explanation (mechanism, data, examples)
- [ ] Minutes 11-14: Implications (what this means + future)
- [ ] Minutes 14-15: Memorable close

### Citations - RIGOROUS
**Every script must have:**
- [ ] 2-3 citations minimum (shorts)
- [ ] 3-4 citations minimum (long-form)
- [ ] Full bibliography at end
- [ ] Formatted consistently (MLA or APA, not random)
- [ ] Sources include: Academic papers, books, company reports, news (not blogs)

**Citation format standard:**
```
Inline: [Author, Year]
Bibliography:
- Piketty, T. (2014). *Capital in the Twenty-First Century.* Belknap Press.
- Federal Reserve. (2023). "Monetary Aggregates Data." Federal Reserve Economic Data.
- McKinsey & Company. (2023). "State of Business Report."
```

**What NOT to cite:**
- Medium articles
- LinkedIn posts
- Twitter threads
- Unverified statistics from unknown sources
- Opinion pieces (unless from notable expert in field)

## PRODUCTION-LEVEL STANDARDS

### Visual Descriptions - SPECIFIC
**Every [VISUAL: X] description must be:**
- [ ] 1-2 sentences (not vague paragraph)
- [ ] Specific about what's on screen (not "building" → "Glass office building, morning light, empty chairs")
- [ ] Includes color palette hints ("bright orange stat on dark blue background")
- [ ] Timing-clear ("This animation holds for 3 seconds")

**Example of VAGUE:** [VISUAL: Business scene]
**Example of EXCELLENT:** [VISUAL: Tesla Gigafactory aerial shot at sunset. Orange/gold light hitting silver building. Assembly line visible through glass. Emphasis: scale and modernity.]

### Audio/Voiceover - NATURAL
**Narrator should:**
- [ ] Sound conversational (not robotic)
- [ ] Use pauses for emphasis (not continuous)
- [ ] Vary tone (not monotone)
- [ ] Speak at 140 words/min (allows breathing)
- [ ] Emphasize key phrases (marked in script)

**Script note format:**
```
"Here's the [EMPHASIS: critical] insight: [pause] Netflix didn't disrupt hotels..."
```

### Text On Screen - READABLE
**Every [TEXT: X] must:**
- [ ] Be readable at 150px width (test on phone)
- [ ] Hold for 2-3 seconds minimum
- [ ] Use high contrast (white on dark, dark on light)
- [ ] Be positioned outside center (where captions auto-appear)
- [ ] Use 1-2 lines (not walls of text)

**Example of GOOD:** [TEXT ON SCREEN: "Platform economics changed everything."]
**Example of BAD:** [TEXT ON SCREEN: "Platform economics, which refers to the way that digital platforms create value by connecting multiple stakeholders, has fundamentally changed..."]

### Color Palette - CONSISTENT
**Brand colors for Executive Forge:**
- Primary: Navy Blue (#001F3F)
- Secondary: Bright Orange (#FF6600)
- Accent: White (#FFFFFF)
- Emphasis: Neon Green (#39FF14)

**Rule:** No more than 3 colors per graphic. Consistency across all videos.

### Graphics/B-Roll - SPECIFIC
**Every [GRAPHICS: X] or [B-ROLL: X] must specify:**
- [ ] What exactly is shown (not "business imagery")
- [ ] Duration (how long this shot holds)
- [ ] Animation style (if applicable)
- [ ] Licensed or created (nothing stolen)

**Example:**
```
[B-ROLL: Airbnb app listing (3 seconds). Show thumbnail, price, reviews. Zoom on $200 nightly rate.]
[GRAPHICS: Network effect animation (6 seconds). Show hosts increasing, guests increasing, overlap arrows growing.]
```

## EDITING-LEVEL STANDARDS

### Pacing
- [ ] Scene changes every 12-15 seconds (maintains engagement)
- [ ] Text holds 2-3 seconds minimum
- [ ] Transitions smooth (fade > cut for educational content)
- [ ] No scene longer than 20 seconds (boring)

### Audio Quality
- [ ] Voiceover clean (no hum, no background noise)
- [ ] Volume consistent throughout
- [ ] Music/background audio enhances without distracting
- [ ] No audio glitches or dropouts

### Visual Quality
- [ ] Resolution consistent (no pixelated shots mixed with 4K)
- [ ] Color grading consistent (color temperature shouldn't jump)
- [ ] Graphics readable on mobile (not tiny text)
- [ ] No watermarks or copyright notices visible

---

## UPLOAD-LEVEL STANDARDS

### Metadata
- [ ] Title: Power word + keyword + intrigue (max 80 characters)
- [ ] Description: Hook sentence, timestamps, resources, CTA
- [ ] Hashtags: 3-5 (niche + trending blend)
- [ ] Thumbnail: High contrast, readable at 150px, 1-2 words max
- [ ] Category: Educational
- [ ] License: Standard YouTube License

### Timestamps
- [ ] Every major section marked
- [ ] Timing accurate (not off by 2 seconds)
- [ ] Chapter names match actual content

### End Screen/Cards
- [ ] Subscribe button always visible
- [ ] Related video suggestion (choose top performer)
- [ ] Link to playlist if applicable
- [ ] No more than 3 interactive elements (they distract)

## CONTENT REVIEW CHECKLIST

**Before a video goes public:**

### Script Review
- [ ] Does hook make you stop scrolling?
- [ ] Is every claim cited?
- [ ] Does prose read smoothly aloud?
- [ ] Does conclusion feel earned?
- [ ] Would you share this with someone smart?

### Visual Review
- [ ] Are descriptions specific enough for a designer?
- [ ] Is color palette consistent?
- [ ] Does B-roll enhance or distract?
- [ ] Is text readable on mobile?
- [ ] Does animation add value?

### Audio Review
- [ ] Does voiceover sound natural?
- [ ] Are key phrases emphasized?
- [ ] Is pacing comfortable (not rushed)?
- [ ] Is background music at right level?
- [ ] Are transitions clean?

### Metadata Review
- [ ] Title is compelling + keyword-rich?
- [ ] Description has hook + value?
- [ ] Thumbnail stands out in feed?
- [ ] Hashtags relevant (not spammy)?
- [ ] CTA is clear but not aggressive?

### Final Check
- [ ] Would you personally watch this?
- [ ] Could you recommend it to a friend?
- [ ] Does it make the channel better?
- [ ] Is it worth the viewer's time?

**If answer to any final check is "no," do not publish.**

---

## WHAT DISQUALIFIES A SCRIPT

**A script is REJECTED if:**

1. **Unverifiable claims** - Even one unverifiable claim kills the script
2. **Weak hook** - If the first 3 seconds don't hook, rewrite
3. **Obvious conclusion** - If the insight is common sense, refine or cut
4. **Weak ending** - If it ends with "and that's why," not a real insight
5. **Tone inconsistency** - If it shifts from serious to jokey to analytical, rewrite
6. **Poor prose** - If it sounds written, not spoken, rewrite
7. **Unclear visuals** - If descriptions are vague, the editor will guess wrong. Reject and clarify.
8. **No citation** - If you can't cite it, don't claim it

## PRODUCTION TIMELINE PER SCRIPT

**For 50-sec short:**
- Writing: 2 hours (research, draft, revise)
- Approval: 1 hour (check facts, refine prose)
- Production: 8 hours (film, edit, color grade, sound mix)
- Review: 2 hours (final check, adjust)
- Total: 13 hours per short

**For 15-min long-form:**
- Writing: 6 hours (research, draft, revise, citations)
- Approval: 2 hours (fact check, detailed review)
- Production: 20 hours (film, edit, graphics, color grade, sound)
- Review: 3 hours (final cuts, optimization)
- Total: 31 hours per long-form

**Budget (rough, if hiring):**
- Shorts: ~$500-800 per script (freelance editor + voiceover)
- Long-form: ~$2K-3K per script (editor + voiceover + graphics)

---

## STANDARDS THAT NEVER CHANGE

1. **Accuracy is non-negotiable.** Wrong information destroys authority faster than any production flaw.

2. **Beautiful prose is foundational.** Scripts should read like smart people talking, not like Wikipedia.

3. **Every piece earns publication.** If it's just "good," it's not good enough. It needs to be "exceptionally useful" or "deeply insightful."

4. **Audience trust is the real currency.** Every decision should ask: "Does this earn or lose audience trust?"

5. **Compounding over virality.** A 100K-view video that teaches something valuable is worth more than a 1M-view viral video that entertains once.
