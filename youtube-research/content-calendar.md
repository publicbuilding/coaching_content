## CONTENT SEQUENCING STRATEGY

### Architecture: "Know-Like-Trust"

**Know Phase (Weeks 1-4):** Entertaining, surprising videos
- Focus: Hooks, curiosity, algorithmic reach
- Example: "Why Netflix Killed Blockbuster" (controversial premise)
- Goal: Get people's attention

**Like Phase (Weeks 5-8):** Educational, deep-dive videos
- Focus: Authority building, expertise demonstration
- Example: Long-form deep dives with data + nuance
- Goal: Make people respect your analysis

**Trust Phase (Weeks 9-12):** Sales-oriented content
- Focus: Course recommendations, soft upsells
- Example: "Here's why you should understand business models [link to course]"
- Goal: Convert to paying customer

### Platform-Specific Optimization

**YouTube Shorts:**
- Best performers (trending hooks): "Why X Collapsed," "How X Became Rich"
- Avg. watch time target: 50%+
- Subscriber conversion rate: 1-2% per video
- Audience: 18-35, interested in business/economics

**Long-Form Videos (15-min):**
- Best performers (narrative depth): Airbnb, Tesla, Costco cases
- Avg. watch time target: 50%+
- Viewer-to-subscriber: 3-5%
- Audience: 25-45, serious about business education

**Cross-Platform Clips:**
- Best repurposing: Scenes with biggest data reveals
- TikTok: Shorter hooks (10-15 sec), trending audio
- Instagram: Aspirational framing ("What I learned from...")
- LinkedIn: Professional angle ("Business leaders should understand...")

## CONTENT BATCHING SCHEDULE

### Production Rhythm (Recommended)
- **Week A:** Film/script 15 shorts + 1 long-form
- **Week B:** Edit and optimize all videos
- **Week C:** Publish 5 shorts from Week A, 1 long-form from Week A
- **Week D:** Continue publishing

**Advantage:** 2-week lag between production and publishing allows for real-time optimization based on data.

## RED FLAGS & PIVOTS

**If shorts aren't hitting 50K views after Week 3:**
- Pivot hook strategy (try "Controversial takes" format)
- Increase upload frequency (test 5 per day)
- Analyze competitor shorts with similar hooks

**If long-form not hitting 200K views by Week 5:**
- Extend length (try 20-25 min instead of 15)
- Add more visual elements (B-roll, graphics)
- Test different thumbnail styles

**If subscriber growth stalls:**
- Do seasonal content audit
- Increase shorts frequency
- Launch "series" (multi-part shorts building tension)

**If revenue is below target:**
- Increase US/UK viewer targeting
- Switch to higher CPM topics
- Accelerate sponsorship outreach

## FINAL NOTES

- **Consistency > Perfection:** A good video published on schedule beats a perfect video published late
- **Data > Intuition:** Let analytics guide decisions, not personal preference
- **Audience First:** Always ask: "Will this help my audience?" before publishing
- **Compounding:** By Week 12, each video will generate views for YEARS. Don't chase trends, build timeless content

**Success Definition for Week 12:**
- 200K+ subscribers
- 1M+ watch hours
- $3K-8K revenue (AdSense + sponsorships)
