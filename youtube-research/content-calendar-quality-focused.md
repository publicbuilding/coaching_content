## RED FLAGS & PIVOTS

**If Week 2 shorts aren't hitting 50K views:**
- Pivot hook (try controversial angles)
- Analyze which week 1 short performed best; base week 3 on that format
- Don't change strategy; refine execution

**If long-forms underperforming:**
- Extend length (try 18-20 min instead of 15)
- Increase B-roll + graphics (make more visual)
- Test different voiceover pacing

**If subscriber growth stalls at Week 6:**
- Check email list: Is it growing? (If yes, audience quality is good, just need more reach)
- Increase shorts frequency (test 3/day instead of 2)
- Launch "clip series" (multi-part shorts building to long-form)

**If revenue below target:**
- Don't panic; focus on subscriber growth
- AdSense CPM varies; geographic targeting helps
- Better to have $5K/month AdSense + $10K/month product interest than $20K/month AdSense + no product interest
