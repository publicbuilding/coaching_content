*8 min read | 2-3 pages*

The foundation of every executive career. Management and leadership are fundamentally different skills, and confusing them will cost you—in retention, performance, and culture.

**What you'll learn:**
- Why your best manager might be failing as a leader
- The core difference in mindset, approach, and outcomes
- How to diagnose which skill you're weaker in

### 2. **Reading Financial Statements in 10 Minutes**
*10 min read | 2-3 pages*

A practical, jargon-free guide to the P&L, Balance Sheet, and Cash Flow Statement. You don't need to be an accountant—you just need to understand what the numbers actually mean.

**What you'll learn:**
- The three statements every executive must read

**Leads to:** Numbers That Matter (masterclass on financial decision-making and strategic analysis)

### 3. **Porter's Five Forces: A 5-Minute Refresher**
*6 min read | 1-2 pages*

Michael Porter's framework for understanding competitive dynamics. Learn why some industries are naturally profitable and others trap you in a margin-destroying race.

**What you'll learn:**
- The five competitive forces that determine profitability
- How to assess each force in your industry
- Why your industry structure matters more than your effort

**Leads to:** Strategic Advantage (course on sustainable competitive positioning)
