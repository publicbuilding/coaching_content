## 🔗 Payment Setup (For Tomorrow)

### Payment Methods Available on Gumroad (Free Tier)

Gumroad handles payments via:
- **Stripe** (primary processor)
- **PayPal** (alternative)
- Automatic payouts to your bank

### Steps to Enable Payments

1. **In Dashboard → Settings → Payout Settings**
2. **Choose Payout Method:**
   - Option A: Stripe (recommended for low-friction setup)
   - Option B: PayPal
3. **Connect Payment Account:**
   - Click "Connect [Payment Service]"
   - Authenticate with Stripe/PayPal
   - Provide banking info for payouts
4. **Tax Compliance (if needed):**
   - US accounts: May need tax ID for 1099-K reporting
   - Non-US: Varies by country
5. **Gumroad Fees:**
   - Free tier: 10% platform fee + payment processor fee (~2.9% + $0.30)
   - Total effective fee: ~13% per sale
   - Recurring subscriptions: 10% platform fee + processor fees

### Payment Link Guide for Tomorrow

**Where to Link Payments:**
1. Each product automatically gets a payment link when published
2. Share links:
   - Individual product links: `gumroad.com/l/[product-slug]`
   - Profile/storefront: `gumroad.com/[username]`
3. Can embed links on:
   - Website (iframe)
   - Email campaigns
   - Social media
   - Forums/communities
