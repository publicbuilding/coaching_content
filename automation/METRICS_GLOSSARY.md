# Metrics Glossary

## Revenue Metrics

### Daily Revenue
**Definition:** Total income generated on a single day across all sources (Gumroad, Substack, email).
**Formula:** Gumroad revenue + Substack revenue + Email revenue
**Why it matters:** Tracks daily performance volatility and sales patterns.

### Weekly Revenue
**Definition:** Sum of all daily revenue for a 7-day period.
**Formula:** SUM(Daily Revenue for 7 days)
**Why it matters:** Smooths out daily noise; identifies weekly trends.

### Monthly Projected Revenue
**Definition:** Estimated monthly revenue based on current daily average.
**Formula:** Daily Average × 30 (or Monthly Revenue to date if we're mid-month)
**Why it matters:** Quick revenue forecast without waiting for month-end.

### Total Revenue (Cumulative)
**Definition:** Sum of all revenue since tracking began.
**Formula:** SUM(All daily revenue)
**Why it matters:** Shows total lifetime earnings; tracks business growth.

### Average Order Value (AOV)
**Definition:** Average revenue per transaction.
**Formula:** Total Revenue / Total Customer Count
**Why it matters:** Identifies pricing strategy effectiveness; guides product strategy.

### Revenue Per Source
**Definition:** Total revenue attributed to each channel.
**Formula:** SUM(revenue for each source type)
**Why it matters:** Identifies highest-performing channels; guides marketing spend.

## Customer & Subscriber Metrics

### Customer Count
**Definition:** Total number of unique customers who made a purchase.
**Why it matters:** Tracks audience growth; used for AOV and LTV calculations.

### Email List Growth
**Definition:** Daily count of email subscribers.
**Why it matters:** Core metric for audience size; predicts future revenue potential.

### Email List Growth Rate (Weekly %)
**Definition:** Weekly percentage change in email subscribers.
**Formula:** (Current Week List Size - Previous Week List Size) / Previous Week List Size × 100
**Why it matters:** Identifies if email marketing efforts are accelerating or stalling.

### Gumroad Followers
**Definition:** Total followers on Gumroad platform.
**Why it matters:** Platform-specific audience metric; indicates product reach.

### Substack Subscribers (Free + Paid)
**Definition:** Free and paid subscriber counts tracked separately.
**Why it matters:** Distinguishes revenue-generating from non-paying audience.

### Twitter/X Followers
**Definition:** Follower count on Twitter/X platform.
**Why it matters:** Social proof; traffic driver to other channels.

### LinkedIn Followers
**Definition:** Follower count on LinkedIn platform.
**Why it matters:** Professional network reach; B2B relevance.

## Conversion Funnel Metrics

### Social Click-Through Rate (CTR)
**Definition:** % of social media followers who click link to email signup.
**Formula:** (Email signups from social / Social clicks) × 100
**Why it matters:** Measures social content effectiveness.

### Email Signup Rate
**Definition:** % of email link clickers who actually sign up.
**Formula:** (Email signups / Email link clicks) × 100
**Why it matters:** Measures landing page/signup form effectiveness.

### Email-to-Buyer Conversion Rate
**Definition:** % of email subscribers who make a purchase.
**Formula:** (Gumroad buyers from email / Email list size) × 100
**Why it matters:** Identifies email list quality; guides email strategy.

### Gumroad Conversion Rate
**Definition:** % of Gumroad visitors who make a purchase.
**Formula:** (Gumroad purchases / Gumroad store visits) × 100
**Why it matters:** Measures product-market fit on Gumroad.

### Revenue Per Email Subscriber (LTV Proxy)
**Definition:** Total revenue generated per email subscriber.
**Formula:** Total Revenue / Current Email List Size
**Why it matters:** Tracks customer lifetime value without long-term data.

## Financial Metrics

### Burn Rate
**Definition:** Cost per day of ad spending or operational expenses.
**Formula:** Total Spend / Days Spending
**Why it matters:** Identifies sustainability; projects runway.

### Return on Ad Spend (ROAS)
**Definition:** Revenue generated per dollar spent on ads.
**Formula:** Revenue from Ad Channel / Ad Spend
**Why it matters:** Determines if ads are profitable.

### Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)
**Definition:** Cost to acquire one customer.
**Formula:** Total Marketing Spend / New Customers Acquired
**Why it matters:** Identifies efficient growth channels.

### Profitability Threshold
**Definition:** Daily revenue needed to break even.
**Formula:** Total fixed costs / Days in period
**Why it matters:** Identifies minimum viable daily revenue.

## Content Performance Metrics

### YouTube Script Engagement Potential
**Definition:** Refinement score indicating likely viewer engagement.
**Scale:** 1-10 (or 1-100 depending on scoring system)
**Why it matters:** Prioritize which scripts to produce first.

### Twitter Post Expected Engagement
**Definition:** Predicted engagement rate based on content, hashtags, timing.
**Formula:** (Expected likes + retweets + replies) / Followers
**Why it matters:** Guides content strategy; identifies high-potential posts.

### Product Sales Rank
**Definition:** Products ranked by total revenue generated.
**Why it matters:** Identifies bestsellers; guides inventory/focus.

### Content-to-Sales Attribution
**Definition:** Track which content pieces drove sales (post URL → revenue).
**Why it matters:** Proves content ROI; identifies best-performing topics.

---

## Projection Metrics

### Week 1 Target vs. Actual
**Definition:** Compare planned revenue goal to actual performance.
**Formula:** Actual / Target × 100 = % of goal achieved
**Why it matters:** Validates projections; adjusts expectations.

### Conservative Revenue Projection
**Definition:** 12-month projection using 80% of current daily average.
**Formula:** (Current Daily Average × 0.8) × 365
**Why it matters:** Realistic baseline for planning.

### Moderate Revenue Projection
**Definition:** 12-month projection using 100% of current daily average.
**Formula:** (Current Daily Average × 1.0) × 365
**Why it matters:** Expected case scenario.

### Aggressive Revenue Projection
**Definition:** 12-month projection assuming 20% month-over-month growth.
**Formula:** Monthly revenue × 12 with compounding growth
**Why it matters:** Best case scenario for planning.

## Quick Reference Table

| Metric | Formula/Input | Update Frequency |
|--------|--------------|------------------|
| Daily Revenue | Gumroad + Substack + Email | Daily |
| Weekly Revenue | SUM(7 days) | Weekly |
| AOV | Total Revenue / Customers | Daily |
| Email List | Manual count | Daily |
| Email Growth % | (New - Old) / Old × 100 | Weekly |
| Email→Buy Conv % | Buyers / List Size × 100 | Daily |
| LTV (proxy) | Total Revenue / List Size | Daily |
| Projections | Daily Avg × 365 (or adjusted) | Weekly |

## Data Entry Checklist

**Every day, log:**
- [ ] Gumroad revenue
- [ ] Gumroad customer count
- [ ] Substack revenue
- [ ] Email revenue (if applicable)
- [ ] Email list size
- [ ] Gumroad follower count

**Every week, log:**
- [ ] Twitter followers
- [ ] LinkedIn followers
- [ ] Substack subscriber breakdown (free/paid)
- [ ] Any ad spend or marketing costs
- [ ] Manual notes on what drove sales

**Monthly review:**
- [ ] Compare projections to actuals
- [ ] Identify top-performing products
- [ ] Review conversion rates
- [ ] Adjust next month's targets
