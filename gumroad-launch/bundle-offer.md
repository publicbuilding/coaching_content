# Bundle Offer Strategy
## Create Urgency & Increase Average Order Value

---

## THE BUNDLE PSYCHOLOGY

**Why bundles work:**
- Higher perceived value (5 products = more value than 1)
- Urgency ("limited time") drives impulse buys
- Removes decision paralysis ("which one should I get?")
- Increases LTV (customer gets more, pays more)
- Creates anchoring effect (compare $149 to $99)

**The math:**
- 5 products @ $29.99 = $149.95 (anchor price)
- Bundle @ $99 = 33% discount
- Feels like a steal = drives conversions

## BUNDLE PERFORMANCE TRACKING

**Metrics to monitor:**

1. **Conversion rate**
   - Bundle buyers / total visitors
   - Target: 3-5% of traffic converts to bundle

2. **Average order value**
   - Track % buying individual vs. bundles
   - Goal: 40% buy bundles (higher AOV)

3. **Bundle attachment rate**
   - % of module buyers who also buy bundle
   - Goal: 20%+ come back for bundle

4. **Repeat purchase rate**
   - % of bundle buyers who buy additional products later
   - Goal: 30%+ buy coaching, accountability, or advanced course

5. **Lifetime value by bundle**
   - Segment revenue by (individual buyer vs. bundle buyer)
   - Bundle buyers likely have 2-3x higher LTV

## COMMON BUNDLE MISTAKES TO AVOID

**❌ Don't make bundles too complicated**
- Stick to 3-5 products per bundle
- More than that = confusing

**❌ Don't discount too much**
- 33% off max (you need margin)
- 50% off looks desperate

**❌ Don't change bundle contents frequently**
- People get confused
- Stick with it for 90 days minimum

**❌ Don't bundle incompatible products**
- "Systems for Startups" + "Enterprise Finance" = confused buyer
- Keep bundles thematic

**❌ Don't promote bundles over individual modules**
- Some people only want 1 module
- Let people choose their path
- Bundles are option, not requirement
