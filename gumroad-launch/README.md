# Gumroad Launch Playbook
## Complete Instructions for Getting 5 Products to Market

**Objective:** Launch 5 executive education modules at $29.99 each on Gumroad, with high conversion pages and strong early sales.

**Timeline:** 4 weeks from prep to launch

**Expected outcomes:** 
- 100+ customers in first 30 days
- $3,000+ revenue in first month
- Foundation for $1,000+/month recurring (via bundles + accountability group)

1. **Email list (if you have one)**
   - Highest conversion (40-60%)

   - Segment by role if possible

2. **LinkedIn**
   - Post about launch in feed
   - Share content snippets ("Here's 3 ways to spot financial red flags")
   - Engage with comments
   - Post testimonials as they come in

3. **Twitter/X**
   - Short updates ("Just launched the financial literacy guide")
   - Share insights from content
   - Retweet social proof
   - Use hashtags (#ExecutiveEducation, #Leadership, etc.)

4. **Reddit**
   - Find relevant subreddits (r/entrepreneurs, r/business, r/Finance)
   - Share your content thoughtfully (not spammy)
   - Engage in discussions
   - Reddit admins often remove self-promo, so be subtle

5. **Your website/blog**
   - Create landing page linking to Gumroad
   - Write blog posts on related topics (link to products)
   - Add products to your main navigation
   - Include product links in email signature

6. **Slack/Discord communities**
   - Join relevant communities in your niche
   - Share when relevant (not constant selling)
   - Help people, mention products when appropriate
   - Build genuine relationships

7. **Personal network (most important)**
   - Email 50 people: "Hey, I just launched something, would love your feedback"
   - Not a sales pitch—genuine ask
   - Early sales = social proof for others

## IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG

**Refund requests:**
- Honor immediately (30-day guarantee)
- Ask why in follow-up (don't argue)
- Use feedback to improve future versions

**Low sales:**
- Check: Is copy clear enough?
- Check: Are testimonials visible?
- Check: Is price reasonable?
- Tweak headlines/bullets and retest

**Tech issues:**
- Gumroad support usually responds within 24h
- Have backup: email people PDF directly if download fails
- Test everything before going live

**Negative feedback:**
- Take it seriously but don't panic
- If 1 person complains, listen
- If 5+ people say same thing, it's real
- Fix quickly and communicate the fix

## Questions You Might Have

**Q: Should I launch all 5 products at once?**
A: Yes. Having 5 creates bundle opportunity + gives customers choice. Don't do them one-by-one.

**Q: What if I'm not famous? Will anyone buy?**
A: Yes. Most Gumroad buyers don't know the creator. They buy because product solves their problem. Social proof (testimonials) matters more than fame.

**Q: Should I offer these on other platforms too?**
A: Start with Gumroad. Once you've proven the model, consider: Teachable, Kajabi, or your own website. One platform first = simpler.

**Q: Can I change prices after launch?**
A: Yes. Don't do it in first 30 days (looks bad). After that, small increases are fine. Communicate the increase.

**Q: What if I get lots of refund requests?**
A: Fix the product. 1-2% refund rate is normal. 10%+ means something's wrong with expectations. Adjust copy/title to set better expectations.

**Q: Should I run ads?**
A: No. Not yet. Get organic traction first. Once you have proven conversion + testimonials, paid ads become profitable. But not week 1.

**Q: How do I reach people interested in this topic?**
A: LinkedIn, email, Twitter, Reddit, Slack communities, your personal network. Start with your network—easiest conversion. Then expand.

**Q: What if no one buys?**
A: Most likely: Your copy isn't clear. Rewrite headlines/benefits. Add more testimonials. Show social proof. Give it 2-3 weeks before panicking.

**Q: Should I offer a free trial or preview?**
A: No. You're offering 30-day money-back guarantee. That's enough. Previews reduce perceived value.
