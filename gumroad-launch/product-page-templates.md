- Keep descriptions scannable (use line breaks, not long paragraphs)

- Lists are easier to read than prose
- Include price in the listing: **$29.99**
- Keep each page under 2,000 words (Gumroad works better with shorter pages)
- Don't include your real name in testimonials (use only initials or first name + initial)
- Link testimonial holders to LinkedIn (adds credibility)
