# Testimonials Request Template
## Email Swipes for Collecting Social Proof

---

## THE STRATEGY

**Goal:** Get 10-20 testimonials before launch (or in first 2 weeks)

**Who to ask:**
- Friends who've benefited from your advice
- Colleagues / mentors who know your work
- Past clients or team members
- People in your network who've faced these problems

**When to ask:**
- ASAP (don't wait for product launch)
- Send 50 requests, expect 5-10 responses
- Give 48-72 hours response time
- Follow up non-responders after 3 days

**What makes good testimonials:**
- ✓ Specific result (not vague praise)
- ✓ 1-2 sentences max (short)
- ✓ Includes name + role/title + company (adds credibility)
- ✓ Emotional + business result
- ✓ Natural language (not salesy)

**What doesn't work:**
- ❌ "This is amazing!" (too vague)
- ❌ Long paragraphs (won't be used)
- ❌ Made-up testimonials (unethical + people know)
- ❌ Only praising, not showing results
- ❌ No name or credentials (worthless for conversion)

## EMAIL TEMPLATE 1: "Product Testimonial Request"

**Subject:** Quick favor: 1-minute testimonial for [Product Name]?

**Body:**

Hey [Name],

I'm launching [Product Name] on Gumroad and would love your perspective.

You've dealt with [specific problem] before. I'm launching a guide to help other executives with the same challenge.

Could you share one quick result from our conversation about [topic]? Just one sentence is perfect.

Something like:
- "I finally understood X. Now I..."
- "Went from [before] to [after]..."
- "Saved X hours/money/sanity by..."

Include your name, title, and company if you're comfortable. LinkedIn link appreciated too (adds credibility).

No pressure if you're busy—totally understand.

—[Your Name]

## THE TESTIMONIAL REQUEST FORM

**If you want more detailed feedback, use this format:**

**Subject:** Testimonial Request: [Product Name]

**Body:**

Quick questions for your testimonial (feel free to answer just 1):

1. **What was the problem you faced?**
   (What was frustrating you before?)

2. **What changed after?**
   (Be specific: "Went from X hours to Y hours" or "Saved $X" or "Confidence went up")

3. **One-liner:**
   Can you summarize your result in one sentence? This is what I'll use.

4. **Name + Title:**
   [Name], [Title], [Company]

5. **LinkedIn profile?**
   (Optional, but adds credibility)

- Problem: "Spent 20 hours a week hiring, making bad decisions"

- Name: Sarah Thompson, VP Operations, TechCorp
- LinkedIn: [url]

*You use:*

## TESTIMONIAL EDITING GUIDE

**Original:** "This course is really great and has helped me understand financial statements and make better decisions."

**Edited:** "Understand financial statements in 30 minutes. Changed how I make decisions."
— Jennifer, CEO

**Why the edit:**
- Removed "really great" (vague)
- Added specificity (30 minutes)
- Removed "helped me understand" (passive)
- Made it benefit-focused (what changed)
- Shortened to one powerful sentence

---

**Original:** "I have been struggling with hiring people for my company for years and this helped me so much."

**Edited:** "Hired 8 people in the last 6 months without a single bad hire. Best $30 I spent."
— Marcus, Founder

**Why the edit:**
- Specific metric (8 people, 6 months, zero bad hires)
- Shows ROI immediately (best $30 spent)
- Removed "struggled for years" (too negative focus)
- Added confidence (he's bragging)

## TESTIMONIAL PLACEMENT ON PAGES

### Where to Put Testimonials

**Location 1: Social Proof Section (Top of page, under headline)**
- Use 3-5 testimonials
- Mix of titles (CEO, VP Ops, Director, etc.)
- Show diversity (different companies, industries if possible)

**Location 2: Mid-Page (After benefits section)**
- Use 2-3 longer testimonials with more context
- Can include photo if available

**Location 3: Before CTA (Right before "Buy Now")**
- Final social proof before purchase decision
- Use strongest, most specific testimonial
- Include credentials prominently

## WHAT TO EXPECT

**Reality check:**
- Send 50 requests → get 5-10 responses (10-20% response rate)
- 50% of responses will be vague → edit them
- 30% will be long → condense them
- 15% will be amazing → use them exactly as written
- 5% will be inappropriate → don't use them

**Timeline:**
- First responses: Day 1-2
- 80% of responses: Day 2-5
- Late responses: Day 10+

**Best response rates:**
- Friends/close network: 40-50%
- Past clients: 25-30%
- Colleagues: 20-25%
- Cold network: 5-10%

---

## AVOIDING FAKE/PROBLEMATIC TESTIMONIALS

**Red flags:**
- ❌ Too perfect / sounds like marketing
- ❌ No verifiable person (initials only, generic "Sarah W.")
- ❌ Vague results with hype language
- ❌ Testimonials that don't match your product
- ❌ Same person giving 3 testimonials

**Always verify:**
- ✓ Real person (full name, title, company)
- ✓ LinkedIn profile exists and matches
- ✓ Sent via email from legitimate email address
- ✓ Specific, measurable results
- ✓ Permission to use publicly

**If someone asks for anonymity:**
- Offer initials + title only: "S.T., VP Operations"
- Or just use first name: "Sarah, COO"
- Still better than nothing
