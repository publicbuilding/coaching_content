---

## Pre-Launch (6-8 weeks before)

1. **Planning:**
   - Define success (how many customers? How much revenue?)
   - Identify launch channels (email, social, PR, partnerships)
   - Create launch budget (marketing spend)

2. **Preparation:**
   - Sales training (they need to sell it)
   - Support training (they'll get questions)
   - Marketing materials (website, one-pager, video)

3. **Beta:**
   - Internal testing
   - Customer testing (feedback loop)
   - Iterate based on feedback

---

## Launch Day

1. **Announcement:** Email + social + PR + direct outreach
2. **Support:** Customer success team ready for questions
3. **Momentum:** Share early wins, testimonials
4. **Follow-up:** Weekly updates on adoption

---

## Post-Launch

1. **Measure:** Did we hit launch goals?
2. **Iterate:** What's working? What's not?
3. **Optimize:** Improve based on feedback
4. **Sustain:** Build momentum (not just one day)

---

## Conclusion

Product launches require planning, coordination, and follow-up. Great launches require all three.
