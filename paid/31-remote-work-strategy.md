## Remote Work Challenges

1. **Isolation:** People feel disconnected
2. **Communication:** Asynchronous is harder
3. **Onboarding:** New hires don't know people
4. **Culture:** Building culture is harder
5. **Collaboration:** Creative work is harder

Success requires intentional systems.

---

## Remote-First Operations

1. **Communication:** Default to written, structured
2. **Meetings:** More structured, shorter
3. **Rituals:** Regular sync-ups build connection
4. **Tools:** Slack for sync, Notion for docs
5. **Culture:** Intentional culture-building

---

## Remote Hiring

Remote work opens up talent pool:
- Hire from anywhere (global talent)
- Longer talent bench (time zones)
- Lower cost (geographic arbitrage)

But remote hiring is harder:
- Assess culture fit without in-person
- No office onboarding
- Less spontaneous connection

---

## Conclusion

Remote work is permanent. Build operations around it. Compete on culture and flexibility.
