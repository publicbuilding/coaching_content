## The Hiring Framework

### Step 1: Define the Role

- What problems solve is this person solving?
- What outcomes are we hiring for?
- What level seniority?

### Step 2: Source Candidates

- Referrals (best source)
- Recruiters (fast but expensive)
- Direct outreach (most work but good)
- Job boards (high volume, lower quality)

### Step 3: Screen

- Phone screen (fit, basics)
- First interview (skills assessment)
- Second interview (culture fit)
- Reference checks (verify track record)

### Step 4: Decision

- Compare candidates
- Reference calls
- Offer

## Hiring Pitfalls

**Pit 1: Hiring for experience instead of fit**

Experience looks good but doesn't guarantee success. Look for problem-solving ability and coachability.

**Pit 2: Hiring fast because you're desperate**

Desperate hiring leads to bad hires. Better to stay understaffed than hire wrong.

**Pit 3: Hiring people like you**

Homogeneous teams perform worse. Hire for diversity of background + thinking.

## Team Scaling

### The Ratio Framework

As you scale, typical ratios:
- 1 manager per 5-8 reports
- 1 director per 2-3 managers
- 1 VP per 2-3 directors

Too many layers = slow. Too few = overwhelmed.
