## The Three Financial Statements

1. **P&L (Income Statement):**
   - Revenue - COGS - OpEx = Profit

2. **Balance Sheet:**
   - Assets = Liabilities + Equity

3. **Cash Flow:**
   - Beginning cash + inflows - outflows = ending cash

---

## Key Financial Ratios

**Gross Margin:** (Revenue - COGS) / Revenue
- Target: 60%+ (SaaS), 40%+ (other)

**Operating Margin:** Operating profit / Revenue
- Target: Path to 20-30%

**Burn Rate:** Monthly cash burn
- Target: <3 years runway

**CAC:** Customer acquisition cost
- Target: Payback in <12 months

---

## Financial Health

Healthy companies:
- Growing revenue >20% YoY
- Improving margins (if path to profitability)
- Positive or reasonable burn rate
- 18+ months runway
