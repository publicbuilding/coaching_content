## Board Meetings

### The Monthly Board Meeting

**Structure:**
- Company metrics review (5 min)
- Strategic update (10 min)
- Problem/decisions (10 min)
- Q&A (10 min)

**Materials:** One-page dashboard + monthly update

### Board Relationships

Board members are advisors and investors. Cultivate relationships:
- Update between meetings (email monthly)
- Seek advice proactively (not just in meetings)
- Be transparent about problems

---

## Fundraising Process

1. **Prepare:** Financial models, pitch deck, investor list
2. **Pitch:** 20 minute pitch + Q&A
3. **Diligence:** Investor investigates further
4. **Term sheet:** Offer to fund
5. **Negotiation:** Terms finalized
6. **Close:** Money in bank

Timeline: 3-6 months

---

## Key Metrics Investors Care About

- Revenue growth rate
- Retention/churn
- Burn rate
- Runway
- Path to profitability

---

## Conclusion

Raise capital strategically. Manage board relationships transparently. Use capital to hit metrics that attract more capital.
