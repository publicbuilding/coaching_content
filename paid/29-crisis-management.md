## The Crisis Management Framework

**Phase 1: Response (First 24 hours)**
- Assess the damage (how bad is it?)
- Communicate internally (show you're in control)
- Take immediate action (contain the crisis)
- Communicate externally (customers, board, investors)

**Phase 2: Resolution (Days 2-7)**
- Solve the root cause
- Regular status updates
- Keep stakeholders informed

**Phase 3: Learning (Weeks 2+)**
- Post-mortem (what did we learn?)
- Process improvements (prevent recurrence)
- Return to normal operations

---

## Types of Crises

- **Technical:** Product down, data loss
- **Personnel:** Key person leaves, scandal
- **Financial:** Cash crisis, investor issue
- **Market:** Disruption, competitor threat
- **Reputational:** Scandal, bad press

Each requires different response. But framework is the same.

---

## Key Principles

1. **Transparency:** Tell the truth about the problem
2. **Action:** Show you're working on the solution
3. **Accountability:** Own the problem (don't blame)
4. **Communication:** Over-communicate, not under-communicate
