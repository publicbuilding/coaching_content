## The Metric Hierarchy

Not all metrics are equal.

**Tier 1: Leading Indicators** (Predict future success)
- Sales pipeline value
- Feature adoption
- Customer NPS

**Tier 2: Lagging Indicators** (Historical results)
- Revenue
- Customer churn
- Profit

**Tier 3: Vanity Metrics** (Look good but don't predict success)
- Page views
- User count (without engagement)
- Total users

Focus on Tier 1 + Tier 2. Ignore Tier 3.

## Building Dashboards

Create one-page dashboard showing:
- Company health (revenue, margin, burn)
- OKR progress
- Key metrics trend (weekly)

Update weekly. Review in all-hands.

## Conclusion

Measure what matters. Dashboard the metrics. Review weekly. Act on insights.
