## What Is Culture?

Culture = How things actually happen in your company

Not what's in the handbook. What's actually true.

---

## Building Strong Culture

1. **Define values** (what do you believe?)
2. **Hire for values** (bring in aligned people)
3. **Reinforce through systems** (reward the behaviors)
4. **Lead by example** (model the culture)
5. **Communicate constantly** (remind people what matters)

---

## Culture Problems to Avoid

1. **Values without behavior:** Say you value integrity but reward ruthlessness
2. **Culture without diversity:** Homogeneous teams can't innovate
3. **Culture without belonging:** People don't feel welcome
4. **Culture without accountability:** Bad behavior isn't addressed

---

## Conclusion

Culture is built intentionally. It takes constant work. Get it right and everything else becomes easier.
