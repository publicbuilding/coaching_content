## What Is Product-Market Fit?

Product-market fit is when:
- Customers are excited about your product
- You can't keep up with demand
- Customers tell others (word of mouth)
- Retention is high
- Revenue is growing fast

---

## Signals of Product-Market Fit

1. **NPS >50** (strong recommendation)
2. **Inbound demand** (customers finding you)
3. **Churn <5%** (customers stay)
4. **Usage is growing** (customers use more over time)
5. **Revenue growth >20% MoM** (early stage) or >10% QoQ (later stage)

If you're at 2-3 of these, you're getting close. At 4-5, you have it.

---

## The Path to Product-Market Fit

1. **Find a customer segment** that has a clear problem
2. **Build MVP** solving that problem
3. **Get early customers** (first 10-50 paying)
4. **Listen obsessively** to feedback
5. **Iterate** based on what you learn
6. **Watch retention** (are customers staying?)
7. **Look for inbound** (are customers pulling your product?)

This takes 12-24 months for most companies.

---

## Post-PMF

Once you have product-market fit:
- Scale marketing (now you know CAC works)
- Scale sales (now you have repeatable sale)
- Build operations (scale without breaking quality)

---

## Conclusion

Product-market fit is critical. Spend time finding it, then scale aggressively.
