## Types of Partnerships

1. **Channel Partnerships:** Partner sells your product
2. **Technology Partnerships:** Integrate each other
3. **Strategic Partnerships:** Co-market, co-develop
4. **Reseller Partnerships:** Partner sells for margin

---

## Partner Selection

Choose partners that:
- Have similar customers (non-overlapping segment)
- Complement your offering (don't compete)
- Have similar quality standards
- Are aligned on business model

---

## Partner Success

1. **Clear agreement:** Contract, SLAs, revenue split
2. **Enablement:** Train partners, give materials
3. **Support:** Help partners succeed
4. **Measure:** Track revenue from partners

---

## Conclusion

Partnerships multiply reach. Select carefully, support actively, measure results.
