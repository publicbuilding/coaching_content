## The CS Lifecycle

**Onboarding:** First 30 days (get to first value)
**Growth:** Months 1-12 (expand usage, upsell)
**Renewal:** Month 12+ (ensure renewal)

Each stage needs different tactics.

---

## CS Metrics

- **Time to value:** Days to first success
- **Adoption:** % of customers using key features
- **Health score:** Prediction of churn (based on usage, support tickets, NPS)
- **NPS:** Customer satisfaction
- **Expansion revenue:** Revenue per customer growth
- **Churn rate:** % of customers leaving

---

## CS Operations

Build systems:
- **Segmentation:** Different customers need different CS (Enterprise vs. SMB)
- **Playbooks:** Standard processes for onboarding, upsell, renewal
- **Automation:** Automated workflows for low-touch segments
- **Dashboards:** Real-time health and progress

---

## Conclusion

CS operations scale customer success. Build processes that work without constant CEO involvement.
