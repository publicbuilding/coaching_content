## Executive Presence Components

1. **Clarity:** You communicate clearly
2. **Conviction:** You believe in what you're saying
3. **Competence:** You know what you're talking about
4. **Composure:** You stay calm under pressure
5. **Consistency:** You behave the same way always

Leaders with presence have all five.

---

## Building Executive Presence

1. **Practice:** Public speaking, presentations
2. **Study:** Learn your domain deeply
3. **Listen:** Understand others before talking
4. **Lead:** Make decisions, take accountability
5. **Develop:** Get feedback, improve
