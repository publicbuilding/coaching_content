## The Decision Matrix

For any decision, evaluate on two dimensions:
- **Impact:** How much does this matter?
- **Uncertainty:** How sure are we of the outcome?

Matrix:
- High Impact + High Uncertainty = Careful analysis, build options, test
- High Impact + Low Uncertainty = Fast decision, clear choice
- Low Impact + High Uncertainty = Quick decision, learn fast
- Low Impact + Low Uncertainty = Delegate or automate

---

## The RACI Framework

For every decision, clarify:
- **R** (Responsible): Who does the work?
- **A** (Accountable): Who's ultimately accountable?
- **C** (Consulted): Whose input is important?
- **I** (Informed): Who needs to know?

Clear RACI = faster, better decisions.

---

## The Decision Deadline

Every decision should have a deadline:
- "We decide Monday at 2pm"

Deadlines force decisions (prevent endless deliberation).
