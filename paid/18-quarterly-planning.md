## The Quarterly Planning Cycle

### Pre-Planning (Week 0)

- Reflect on last quarter (what worked? What didn't?)
- Analyze market and competitive changes
- Review financial position
- Brainstorm opportunities for next quarter

### Planning (Week 1)

- Leadership defines company OKRs
- Teams define team OKRs
- Align OKRs across teams

### Kickoff (Week 2)

- Publish OKRs company-wide
- Each person understands their role
- Set expectations for weekly check-ins

### Execution (Weeks 3-13)

- Work toward OKRs
- Weekly check-ins on progress
- Mid-quarter adjustments if needed

### Review (Week 14)

- Assess quarterly results
- Celebrate wins
- Plan next quarter

---

## Resource Allocation

For each OKR, allocate resources:
- Headcount
- Budget
- Tools/systems

Make sure resources are sufficient to achieve the OKR.

---

## Conclusion

Quarterly planning translates strategy into execution. The cycle is: Plan → Execute → Review → Repeat.
