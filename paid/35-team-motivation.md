## The Motivation Framework

**Autonomy:** Do people have control over their work?
**Mastery:** Are they getting better at what they do?
**Purpose:** Do they believe in the mission?

Teams with all three are highly motivated.

---

## Motivation Killers

1. **Micromanagement:** No autonomy kills motivation
2. **No growth:** No mastery opportunities kills motivation
3. **No purpose:** Not believing in mission kills motivation
4. **Unfair compensation:** Resentment kills motivation
5. **No recognition:** No acknowledgment kills motivation

---

## Building Motivation

1. **Clear goals:** People know what success looks like
2. **Autonomy:** Tell them what, not how
3. **Growth:** Invest in their development
4. **Recognition:** Celebrate wins
5. **Fairness:** Equitable compensation, opportunities
