## Types of Competitors

**Direct:** Sell the same solution to same customers
**Indirect:** Solve the same problem differently
**Future:** Don't exist yet but could disrupt

Focus on direct. Keep eye on future.

---

## Competitive Advantages

Sustainable advantages:
- **Scale:** Only you can do it at volume/cost
- **Network effects:** More users = more valuable
- **Switching costs:** Hard to leave once in
- **Brand:** Customers prefer you
- **IP/Patents:** Legally protected differentiation

---

## Competitive Positioning

Don't compete on features (copied quickly). Compete on:
- **Value to customer** (you save them more money/time)
- **Customer fit** (you serve their needs better)
- **Brand trust** (they believe you more)

---

## Beating Competitors

1. **Know your competition** (what are they good at? Bad at?)
2. **Find your advantage** (where can you win?)
3. **Play to that advantage** (don't fight where they're stronger)
4. **Make it defensible** (build something they can't copy)

---

## Conclusion

Competitive strategy is about finding your advantage and playing to it relentlessly.
