---

## Enterprise Sales Challenges

- **Long sales cycles** (6+ months)
- **Multiple stakeholders** (10-20 people making the decision)
- **Complex implementations** (not self-serve)
- **High support requirements** (not a product, it's a partnership)

Success requires different sales, product, and ops.

---

## Enterprise Sales Process

1. **Prospecting:** Find champion inside company
2. **Discovery:** Understand needs (and politics)
3. **Proposal:** Show how you solve their problems
4. **Negotiations:** Usually long and complex
5. **Implementation:** Usually 3-6 months
6. **Expansion:** Land and expand

Timeline: 9-18 months from first conversation to closed deal.

---

## Enterprise Metrics

- **ACV:** $100K+ (vs. $1K+ for SMB)
- **Sales cycle:** 6+ months
- **Implementation:** 3-6 months
- **Support:** Dedicated success manager

---

## Challenges to Avoid

1. **Customization:** Too much custom work kills margins
2. **Long implementation:** Delays revenue recognition
3. **Scope creep:** Customers add features during implementation
4. **Support burden:** Enterprise customers need more support

---

## Conclusion

Enterprise is high-value but requires different playbook. Only pursue if economics work (ACV >> CAC).
