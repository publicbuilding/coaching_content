## STRUCTURE FOR ALL 12

### Opening (0:00-1:30) - HOOK + PREMISE
- Bold claim that breaks intuition
- Example: "Netflix killed Blockbuster but Blockbuster had 9,000 stores"
- Makes viewer go "wait, how?"

### Context (1:30-4:00) - WHY THIS HAPPENED
- Historical setup showing conventional wisdom
- Shows what people got wrong
- Example: "Hotels optimized for scale, not experience"

### Analysis (4:00-11:00) - THE MECHANISM
- Deep explanation of how incentives work
- Data-driven proof
- Shows second and third-order effects
- Example: "Network effects + switching costs = moat"

### Implication (11:00-14:00) - SO WHAT?
- What this means for similar businesses
- What principle transfers to other industries
- Future prediction (if relevant)

### Conclusion (14:00-15:00) - MEMORABLE CLOSE
- Recap the insight (1-2 sentences)
- Actionable principle
- Example: "Don't compete with disruption. Anticipate it."

## PRODUCTION STANDARDS

### Citations - ACADEMIC RIGOR
- [ ] Every major claim has [Author, Year]
- [ ] Sources are primary (studies, reports, books) not secondary (Medium articles)
- [ ] At least 1 citation per 400 words
- [ ] Bibliography is complete + formatted (MLA/APA consistent)
- [ ] Publication dates are recent (2015+) unless foundational (Christensen 1997 OK)

### Prose - LITERARY QUALITY
- [ ] Opening paragraph makes reader lean in
- [ ] Explanations use metaphor/analogy (not just theory)
- [ ] No jargon without definition
- [ ] Sentence rhythm varies (short. Long longer sentences. Medium.)
- [ ] Conclusion feels earned, not forced

### Data Presentation
- [ ] Every statistic has source
- [ ] Numbers are exact (not rounded misleadingly)
- [ ] Comparisons are fair (apples-to-apples)
- [ ] Outliers/exceptions are acknowledged
- [ ] Data visualized in script (clear what B-roll/graphics show)

### Narrator Notes
- [ ] Voiceover script is conversational (not written-for-reading)
- [ ] Pacing: 140 words/min (allows breathing)
- [ ] Emphasis marked for key phrases
- [ ] Tone consistent (not flip-flopping between serious/sarcastic)

### Visual Specifications
- [ ] [VISUAL] descriptions are 1-2 sentences, specific
- [ ] [TIMING] shows exactly when to transition
- [ ] [GRAPHICS] describes what chart/animation needed
- [ ] [B-ROLL] references are specific (not just "stock footage")
