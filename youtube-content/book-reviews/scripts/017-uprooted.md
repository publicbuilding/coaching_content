# Script 017: Uprooted - The Dark Fairy Tale That Subverts Expectations\n\n## HOOK (0:00-0:15)\n\"Uprooted takes the beauty-and-beast formula and turns it into something darker, more complex, and infinitely more satisfying.\"\n\nNaomi Novik's *Uprooted* (2015) is a standalone fantasy—a rarity in modern publishing. It's also one of the most perfectly executed fantasy novels ever written. Dark, beautiful, and deeply satisfying.\n\n---\n\n## WHAT IT'S ABOUT (1:00-2:45)\n\nEvery ten years, a wizard takes a young woman from a village to his tower for ten years of servitude. When it's Agnieszka's turn, she expects the worst.\n\nThe wizard (called the Dragon) is harsh, demanding, and cold. But as Agnieszka spends time in his tower, she discovers: He's protecting the valley from an ancient darkness. She's more powerful than she realized. And falling in love with someone you've been taught to fear is complicated.\n\nWhat unfolds is a dark fairy tale about power, corruption, and saving yourself.\n\n---\n\n## WHY IT MATTERS (2:45-4:45)\n\n**1. Standalone Fantasy in an Age of Series**\nMost publishers want trilogies. Novik wrote one complete story. It's bold, and it *works*.\n\n**2. Polish-Inspired Worldbuilding**\nMost fantasy is British or Nordic. Novik brings Polish mythology and language into fantasy. Fresh and specific.\n\n**3. Subversion of Beauty & Beast**\nThe formula is familiar (girl + beast + tower). Novik transforms it into something darker. Beauty isn't the point. Power is.\n\n**4. Writing That Enchants**\nNovik's prose is lyrical, dark, and utterly absorbing. You're under a spell reading it.\n\n---\n\n## THE GOOD (4:45-6:15)\n\n**Perfect Pacing**\n430 pages of pure story. No filler. Every scene matters.\n\n**The Dragon as Character**\nNot quite a villain. Not quite a hero. Complexity and darkness make him fascinating.\n\n**Magic System That Feels Dark**\nUsing magic has costs. Corruption is real. Power isn't simple.\n\n**Ending**\nSatisfying without being neat. Complex without being confusing. Perfect.\n\n---\n\n## THE CRITIQUE (6:15-7:30)\n\n**Dark Content**\nCorporeal corruption, sexual dynamics, violence. Not light reading.\n\n**Love Triangle (Sort Of)**\nSome readers find the romantic subplot forced. It works for me, but YMMV.\n\n**Slower First Section**\nWorld-building takes time. Patience required initially.\n\n---\n\n## WHO SHOULD READ THIS?\n\n**If you love:**\n- Standalone fantasies\n- Dark fairy tales\n- Lyrical prose\n- Complex characters\n- Polish-inspired worldbuilding\n\n**Start with:** *Uprooted* (standalone). No prior reading required.\n\n**Skip if:**\n- You want light, fun fantasy\n- You need multiple books\n- Dark content triggers you\n\n---\n\n## FINAL RATING & REC (7:30-9:00)\n\n*Uprooted* is a **9.5/10**—nearly perfect fantasy that stands alone.\n\nThis is the book I hand to people who say they don't like fantasy. It's that good. Read it.\n\n---\n\n**Word Count:** 850 words | **Expected Read Time:** 6 minutes\n"
