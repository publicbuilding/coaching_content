# YouTube Shorts: Book Review Highlights

## STRATEGY
Extract the most compelling 60-90 second segments from each video script.
Each short has:
- **Hook** (5-10 sec): Grabbing opening
- **Insight** (30-40 sec): Key point or quote
- **CTA** (10-15 sec): Call-to-action (full video link)

**Platform Specs:**
- Format: Vertical (9:16)
- Length: 60-90 seconds
- Text overlays: 2-3 key phrases
- Audio: Engaging, energetic (faster pace than full video)

## PRODUCTION NOTES

**Audio Design:**
- Hook: Punchy, attention-grabbing (0-10 sec)
- Insight: Engaging narration, conversational pace
- CTA: Energetic, encouraging click

**Visual Elements:**
- Book cover (1-2 sec intro)
- Author photo/relevant imagery (10-15 sec)
- Key quotes on-screen (15-30 sec)
- Thematic visuals (30-45 sec)
- Call-to-action screen (5-10 sec)

**Text Overlays:**
- 1 main title (hook)
- 2-3 key phrases (insight)
- Video link (CTA)

**Hashtag Strategy:**
- Primary: Book title + author
- Secondary: Genre tags (#GrimdarkFantasy, #HardSciFi)
- Tertiary: Engagement tags (#BookTok, #BookTube)

---

## DISTRIBUTION PLAN

**YouTube Shorts Tab:**
- Upload 3-5 shorts per day
- Stagger uploads (don't flood)
- Cross-promote with full videos

**TikTok:**
- Same shorts, TikTok-optimized (music, trending sounds)
- Shorter hooks (5-7 sec)
- More text overlays

**Instagram Reels:**
- Slower pacing (longer clips)
- More visuals
- Cross-link to YouTube

**Twitter/X:**
- Quote graphics from shorts
- Discussion prompts
- Book cover art
