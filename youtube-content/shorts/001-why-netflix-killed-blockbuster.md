## SCRIPT

**[VISUAL: Bold text overlay on dark background]**
"Blockbuster had 9,000 stores. Netflix had zero."

**[AUDIO: Narrator voiceover + trending sound]**
"Yet Netflix is worth $200 billion. Blockbuster is dead. Here's why."

---

**[VISUAL: Quick cut - old Blockbuster store exterior]**
**[AUDIO:]** "Blockbuster made money from late fees. That was the business model. Every time you returned a movie late, they won."

**[TEXT ON SCREEN: "Late Fee Revenue: 20% of Blockbuster's Income"]** *(Cites: Sager, 2010)*

---

**[VISUAL: Netflix logo, red envelope animation]**
**[AUDIO:]** "Netflix did something radical: they eliminated late fees entirely."

**[TEXT ON SCREEN: "Netflix Monthly Subscription: Unlimited Returns"]**

---

**[VISUAL: Graph showing subscriber growth vs. late fee model]**
**[AUDIO:]** "By removing friction, they made customers love them. Blockbuster couldn't copy it—their profits depended on late fees."

**[TEXT ON SCREEN: "The Paradox: Blockbuster's Strength Was Its Fatal Weakness"]** *(Cites: Christensen, The Innovator's Dilemma, 1997)*

---

**[VISUAL: Netflix logo grows larger, Blockbuster fades]**
**[AUDIO:]** "This is called 'business model innovation.' You don't beat competitors by doing what they do better. You beat them by changing the game entirely."

**[TEXT ON SCREEN: "Save This Breakdown"]** *(Last 3 seconds)*
