# THE EXECUTIVE FORGE - NEWSLETTER TEMPLATE & FORMAT

## Template Structure

Each weekly newsletter follows this exact format:

---

## [HEADER - DO NOT MODIFY]

**THE EXECUTIVE FORGE**  
Where Leaders Are Made

*Issue #[NUMBER] • [DATE]*

---

## [SECTION 1: OPENING - THE REAL TALK (50-100 words)]

Personal observation, current event, or leadership moment that sets the tone for the issue. This is your voice. Make it real.

**Example structure:**
- Lead with a story or moment of truth
- End with the question the week's insight will answer
- Create curiosity but stay grounded

---

## [SECTION 2: THE MAIN INSIGHT (300-500 words)]

**[BOLD TITLE]**

This is the meaty section. The framework, strategy, or insight that makes your readers think differently.

**Structure:**
1. Problem statement (what's wrong with how leaders usually think)
2. Why it matters (what's at stake)
3. The framework/principle (the actual insight)
4. How to apply it (concrete steps or questions to ask yourself)
5. What happens when you do this (the payoff)

**Tone:**
- Direct and unfiltered
- Use examples, not abstractions
- Assume intelligence but no domain knowledge
- Back claims with evidence or experience
- End with actionable next steps

---

## [SECTION 3: FROM THE FORGE - QUICK TIP (50-100 words)]

**FROM THE FORGE**

A tactical, immediately-usable tip. This should be:
- One specific thing you can implement this week
- Self-contained (doesn't require the main article)
- Practical enough that someone can do it in their next meeting
- Related to executive presence, decision-making, or influence

**Example format:**
"When you're in a meeting and need to build credibility fast: Ask one question that makes someone else *smarter*. People remember who made them think, not who talked the most."

---

## [SECTION 4: FROM THE PAID TIER - TEASER (1-2 paragraphs)]

**THIS WEEK IN THE FORGE (Premium Exclusive)**

Give them a taste of what's in the paid tier. This isn't a sales pitch—it's proof that premium content is deeper/richer.

**Format:**
- Title of this week's premium piece
- 1-2 sentence teaser
- "This framework applies to [specific scenario]"
- NO link or CTA yet (we want to build anticipation, not push sales)

---

## [FOOTER SECTION]

---

**The Executive Forge** is a weekly letter on leadership strategy, decision-making, and rare perspectives from leaders who've been in the arena.

Free weekly insights every [DAY]. Premium access to frameworks, case studies, and community access at [LINK].

Questions? Reply to this email. I read everything.

—[Your Name]

[SOCIAL LINKS IF APPLICABLE]

---

## DESIGN NOTES

- Serif font for headers (professional, strong)
- Clean spacing between sections
- Bold for emphasis, never ALL CAPS
- Use [COLOR: RED #D32F2F] for section headers and key terms
- Keep line length readable (60-80 characters)
- Mobile-first thinking (test on phone)

## PUBLISHING CADENCE

- **Free tier:** Once per week, [SELECTED DAY] at [SELECTED TIME]
- **Premium tier:** Same day + bonus weekly deep-dive workshop video
- **Consistency is key** — same day, same time builds habit

## TONE & VOICE

- Direct but never harsh
- Confident but never cocky
- Expert but never condescending
- Use "you" frequently (talk to the reader)
- Use contractions (it's, don't, won't) — conversational
- Stories over statistics
- Experience over theory
- Questions over statements (to provoke thinking)

## WHAT NOT TO DO

❌ Never apologize for frequency or missed issues  
❌ Never use marketing-speak ("sign up now!", "limited time!")  
❌ Never ask for shares or likes  
❌ Never republish content from other sources without transformation  
❌ Never write about topics you haven't personally experienced  
❌ Never send without one final read-through for clarity  

---

This template ensures every issue feels like it comes from one human to another, not from a brand to an audience.
