## 🎯 Pricing Strategy Rationale

### Why $29.99 for Individual Modules?
- **Price point:** Below $30 (psychological pricing)
- **Accessibility:** Impulse-buy range for target audience
- **Bundling incentive:** Creates natural upsell to $199.99 core bundle
- **Premium positioning:** Not too cheap (implies quality)
- **Comparable:** Similar to online course platforms (Udemy, MasterClass single courses)

### Why $199.99 for Core Bundle?
- **Value proposition:** 33% discount vs. buying all separately ($299.90)
- **Price anchor:** Creates perceived value and urgency
- **Sweet spot:** Affordable for individual executives; high enough to indicate quality
- **Comparison:** Similar to LinkedIn Learning annual ($240/year) or similar B2B courses

### Why $59.99 for Industry Deep Dive?
- **Entry point:** For price-sensitive evaluators
- **Speed:** Complete in 3-4 weeks vs. 8-10 weeks for full bundle
- **Testing:** Lower commitment = higher conversion on trial
- **Add-on:** Creates funnel (buy starter → buy complete library later)

### Why $399.99 for Complete Library?
- **Premium tier:** For serious executives/teams
- **High-ticket feel:** Indicates comprehensive, premium content
- **ROI positioning:** Cost is 0.1% of leader's annual salary/income
- **Masterclass bonus:** Exclusive content justifies premium price
- **Team purchase:** Companies buying for multiple employees

### Why $14.99/Month for Subscription?
- **Recurring revenue:** Builds predictable monthly cash flow
- **Low commitment:** Easy recurring sell (almost impulse)
- **Community value:** Ongoing engagement, not one-time purchase
- **Comparable:** Similar to newsletter subscriptions, coaching memberships
- **Churn tolerance:** Low enough price that customers don't cancel immediately

## 📈 Conversion Optimization

### Product Page Optimization
1. **Hero Section:** Use brand colors (deep red, gold)
2. **Module Cover Images:** Professional, consistent design
3. **Benefit-Focused Copy:** Not features, but outcomes
4. **Social Proof:** Testimonials, student count (once you have customers)
5. **Pricing Display:** Show original vs. bundle savings
6. **CTA Button:** Clear "Buy Now" or "Get Access"

### Bundling Psychology
- Always show bundle savings in percentage terms
- Use tiered pricing (3 options: cheap, medium, premium)
- Make the middle option look best (Core Bundle)
- Show what customers get explicitly
