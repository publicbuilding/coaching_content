## 💡 PRO TIPS

### Engagement Boosters
- Respond to EVERY comment/reply for first 48 hours
- Retweet replies with kind comments
- Answer questions about the framework
- Build relationship, not just broadcast

### If Post Isn't Converting
- Check your link (does it work?)
- Check the headline (is it clear?)
- Check the angle (is it resonating?)
- Retweet the same post with different copy

### If Post Is Converting Well
- Retweet it again (people miss things first time)
- Reference it in follow-up posts
- Double down on that angle

### Timing Notes
- 9 AM works for US morning professionals
- If your audience is Europe, try 8 AM ET / 1 PM GMT
- Friday often gets lower engagement (people checking out)
- Tuesday-Thursday usually best

## 🚨 WHAT NOT TO DO

❌ **Don't be salesy.** "Buy my thing now" performs worse than "here's something useful."

❌ **Don't post too often.** Once per day is enough. More = annoying.

❌ **Don't ignore comments.** Every reply is a chance to connect.

❌ **Don't generic post.** "Excited to launch!" means nothing. Show your work.

❌ **Don't delete old posts.** They keep working. Retweet them instead.

✅ **Do be helpful first.** Teaching > selling.

✅ **Do tell stories.** People connect to narratives, not product features.

✅ **Do be specific.** "First 90 days" beats "leadership tips."

✅ **Do engage.** Build a community, not an audience.
