## 💰 Revenue Math (Conservative)

- **Landing page conversion rate:** 20% of clicks → emails (10 sign-ups per 50 clicks)
- **Email to purchase:** 10% of subs buy (10 purchases from 100 subs)
- **Average order value:** $49 (mix of $29 and $79 products)
- **Math:** 100 subs × 10% = 10 sales × $49 = **$490 revenue**

**To hit $800:**
- Need ~150 email subscribers (150 × 10% × $49 = $735) ✓
- OR 15 sales at $49 avg = $735 ✓
- Doable if landing page gets 500+ clicks across the week

**Traffic sources (estimate 500+ clicks):**
- Twitter: 200-300 clicks (high-engagement network)
- LinkedIn: 100-150 clicks (professional audience)
- Personal email blast: 50-100 clicks (day 1 + Gumroad launch)
- Organic: 50 clicks (if you engage in communities)

**Total:** 400-600 clicks = 80-120 email subs = $400-600 revenue ✓

## 🎬 GO LIVE MINDSET

- **Momentum builds:** First 5 sales give you credibility. First 50 subs give you audience. Build on that.
- **This is week 1.** You're not building the final product. You're proving demand and getting initial revenue. That's it.
