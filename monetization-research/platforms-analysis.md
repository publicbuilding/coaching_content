# Platform Analysis: Executive Forge Monetization Options

## Executive Summary
Seven platforms analyzed for Executive Forge (business/executive education). Best fits: **Teachable** (courses) + **Substack** (newsletter) + **Mighty Networks** (community) + **Kajabi** (all-in-one).

---

## 1. SUBSTACK (Newsletter + Paid Tiers)

### Platform Overview
Substack is a creator platform focused on newsletters and writing with built-in monetization.

### Pricing & Revenue Share
- **Setup Cost:** FREE (free tier available forever)
- **Revenue Share:** You keep **90%** after credit card processing fees
- **Transaction Processing:** Standard credit card fees apply (~2-3%)
- **Net to Creator:** ~87-88% of subscription revenue

### Features
- Free newsletter publishing
- Paid subscriber tiers (monthly, annual subscriptions)
- Email delivery infrastructure included
- Basic analytics dashboard
- Subscriber management tools
- Archive & discoverability (minimal)

### Executive Forge Fit: **HIGH**
- **Setup:** Easiest - no setup cost, immediate launch
- **Audience Expectations:** 
  - Niche industry content: $5-20/month
  - Advanced frameworks: $10-50/month
  - Exclusive insights/case studies: $15-100/month
- **Integration:** Excellent with YouTube (drive paid newsletter subscribers from free content)
- **Effort:** Low - 1-2 hours/week for newsletter creation
- **Revenue Potential:** 
  - Conservative: 100 subscribers @ $10/mo = $1,000/mo ($10,800/yr)
  - Moderate: 250 subscribers @ $15/mo = $3,750/mo ($45,000/yr)
  - Aggressive: 500 subscribers @ $20/mo = $10,000/mo ($120,000/yr)

### Comparison with ConvertKit
ConvertKit focuses on email marketing with monetization add-ons:
- **ConvertKit Pricing:** $33/mo (Creator), $66/mo (Pro)
- **Revenue Share:** Takes 23.5% on "Paid Recommendations"
- **Verdict:** Substack beats ConvertKit for pure paid newsletter (90% vs 76.5%)

### Action Items
- **Month 1:** Set up Substack, migrate newsletter subscribers from current list
- **Month 2:** Introduce paid tier ($10-15/mo) with exclusive weekly insights
- **Month 3+:** Growth through YouTube CTAs and sponsorship mentions

## 2. MAVEN (Live Cohort Courses)

### Platform Overview
Maven specializes in live, instructor-led cohort courses. Fresh platform gaining traction with creators.

### Pricing & Revenue Share
- **Setup Cost:** FREE
- **Revenue Share:** Maven takes ~30% commission
- **Net to Creator:** ~70% (competitive for live courses)
- **Typical Course Price:** $300-$2,000+ per cohort
- **Cohort Size:** 20-500 students per cohort

### Features
- Live cohort management (automated scheduling)
- Video streaming built-in
- Community/discussion forums
- Certificate of completion
- Automated cohort enrollment
- Analytics on student progress
- Certification features
- Zero upfront cost

### Executive Forge Fit: **HIGH**
- **Setup:** Moderate - requires course structure design (~2 weeks prep)
- **Audience Expectations:**
  - Short workshops (4-week cohorts): $297-$597
  - Extended programs (8-week cohorts): $597-$1,497
  - Premium cohorts: $1,497-$2,997
- **Integration:** Perfect for hosting live executive cohorts mentioned in YouTube/sponsorship content
- **Effort:** High during cohort (5-10 hours/week for 4-8 weeks)
- **Revenue Potential:**
  - Conservative: 1 cohort/quarter × 30 students × $500 × 70% = $10,500/quarter ($42,000/yr)
  - Moderate: 2 cohorts/quarter × 50 students × $750 × 70% = $52,500/quarter ($210,000/yr)
  - Aggressive: 2 cohorts/quarter × 75 students × $1,200 × 70% = $126,000/quarter ($504,000/yr)

### Why Maven Over Teachable/Kajabi for Cohorts
- Maven is optimized specifically for cohort-based courses
- Lower commission than Teachable/Kajabi for this use case
- Built-in peer engagement (critical for cohort success)
- No infrastructure management needed

### Action Items
- **Month 1-2:** Design 4-week "Executive Leadership Cohort" curriculum
- **Month 3:** Soft launch cohort to YouTube audience (first 30 students at $397)
- **Month 4+:** Scale to 2 cohorts per quarter

## 3. TEACHABLE (Full Course Platform)

### Platform Overview
Established, all-in-one course hosting with heavy customization and affiliate program.

### Pricing & Revenue Share
- **Setup Cost:** $39-$399/month depending on plan
- **Transaction Fees:** 
  - Starter: 7.5% (higher cost)
  - Builder & above: 0% (paid products)
  - Plus standard credit card processing (2.9% + $0.30)
- **Net to Creator:** ~90-92% of course revenue on paid plans

### Plans Comparison
| Plan | Cost | Products | Best For |
|------|------|----------|----------|
| Starter | $39/mo | 1 product | Testing |
| Builder | $89/mo | 5 products | Growing |
| Growth | $189/mo | 25 products | Scaling |
| Advanced | $399/mo | 100 products | Enterprise |

### Features
- White-labeled website
- Video hosting (up to 1TB)
- Drip courses & lesson scheduling
- Built-in email marketing
- Affiliate program (for students to refer)
- Quizzes & certificates
- Community forum
- Automations
- API access (higher plans)
- Mobile apps (iOS/Android)
- Integrations with Zapier, etc.

### Executive Forge Fit: **HIGH**
- **Setup:** Moderate - course creation + platform config (3-4 weeks)
- **Audience Expectations:**
  - Self-paced courses: $97-$297
  - Advanced programs: $297-$997
  - Multi-course bundles: $997-$2,997
- **Integration:** Excellent - can be promoted from YouTube, email, other channels
- **Effort:** Medium - varies with course scope (5-15 hours/week depending on creation phase)
- **Revenue Potential:**
  - Conservative: 200 course sales × $200 × 90% = $36,000/yr
  - Moderate: 500 course sales × $250 × 90% = $112,500/yr
  - Aggressive: 1,000 course sales × $300 × 90% = $270,000/yr

### Why Teachable is Strong
- Established, trusted platform (10+ years)
- Excellent affiliate program built-in (add passive revenue)
- Clean pricing: no transaction fees on paid plans
- Mobile apps increase student engagement
- White-labeled option (brand control)

### Action Items
- **Month 1-3:** Create flagship "Executive Leadership Masterclass" (10-15 modules)
- **Month 4:** Launch with YouTube promotion
- **Month 5+:** Add supplementary courses (Finance, Communication, etc.)

## 4. CIRCLE (Membership Community)

### Platform Overview
Community platform focused on member engagement with built-in courses, events, and monetization.

### Pricing & Revenue Share
- **Setup Cost:** $89-$199/mo + optional Email Hub ($99/mo)
- **Revenue Model:** You set pricing for memberships (no commission)
- **Net to Creator:** ~96-97% (only credit card processing fees)
- **Typical Membership Price:** $25-$150/month

### Plans
| Plan | Cost | Features |
|------|------|----------|
| Professional | $89/mo | Courses, discussions, events, live streams, custom branding |
| Business | $199/mo | Workflows, API access, custom fields, advanced analytics |
| Circle Plus | Custom | AI agents, SSO, priority support, branded apps |

### Features
- Unlimited members
- Courses module
- Discussion forums
- Live streams & events
- Gamification (points, badges)
- Custom branding
- Rich member profiles
- Activity feeds
- Analytics & reporting
- Automated workflows (Business plan+)
- Optional branded mobile app

### Executive Forge Fit: **VERY HIGH**
- **Setup:** Moderate - community design + initial content (2-3 weeks)
- **Audience Expectations:**
  - Community access (no courses): $25-$50/month
  - Community + courses: $50-$100/month
  - VIP + 1-on-1 access: $100-$300/month
- **Integration:** Excellent - drives engagement and repeat revenue
- **Effort:** Moderate - 5-8 hours/week for community management
- **Revenue Potential:**
  - Conservative: 50 members × $50/mo = $2,500/mo ($30,000/yr)
  - Moderate: 150 members × $75/mo = $11,250/mo ($135,000/yr)
  - Aggressive: 300 members × $100/mo = $30,000/mo ($360,000/yr)

### Unique Advantages for Executive Forge
- Member-to-member engagement (executives networking)
- Recurring revenue (highest lifetime value)
- Built-in courses + community (two revenue streams in one)
- Analytics on member activity & retention
- Branded mobile app option (increases stickiness)

### Action Items
- **Month 1:** Design community structure (exclusive forums, monthly themes, events)
- **Month 2:** Launch "Executive Forge Circle" at $69/month with 20 founding members (discounted)
- **Month 3+:** Growth through YouTube, email, partnerships

| Plan | Cost | Best For |
|------|------|----------|
| Launch | $950/yr | Startups, first community |

| Growth | Custom | Scaling (2,000+ members) |

- Challenges & mini-courses
- Live workshops
- Discussion forums
- Member-led activity (84% of content from members)

- Community Design™ framework

- Automated engagement (onboarding, recognition, re-engagement)

- **Audience Expectations:**

- **Revenue Potential:**
  - Conservative: 10 members × $48/mo = $480/mo ($5,760/yr)
  - Moderate: 50 members × $60/mo = $3,000/mo ($36,000/yr) + $950 setup
  - Aggressive: 200 members × $79/mo = $15,800/mo ($189,600/yr)

### Why Mighty Networks Stands Out
- ONLY platform with native mobile apps included (60% engagement boost)
- AI Cohost reduces operational burden
- Community Design™ framework (proven to work)
- $500M earned on platform in 2025 (validation)
- Best for engagement-first communities

### Caution
- Annual commitment ($950) vs. monthly plans elsewhere
- Best for communities that WILL activate (not just passive learning)

### Action Items

## 6. KAJABI (All-in-One Platform)

### Platform Overview
Comprehensive platform for courses, memberships, email marketing, landing pages, and more.

### Pricing & Revenue Share
- **Setup Cost:** $143-$399/month (billed annually, discounted from monthly)
- **Revenue Model:** You set pricing (no commission from Kajabi)
- **Payment Processing:** 2.7-2.9% + $0.30 (Kajabi Payments) or 0.5-2% (3rd party)
- **Net to Creator:** ~97-98%

### Plans
| Plan | Cost/mo | Products | Contacts | Best For |
|------|---------|----------|----------|----------|
| Basic | $143 | 5 | 2,500 | Small launch |
| Growth | $199 | 50 | 25,000 | Scaling |
| Pro | $399 | Unlimited | 100,000 | Enterprise |

### Features
- Unlimited marketing emails & landing pages
- Course builder with AI
- Membership/community platform
- Funnels & automations
- Website builder
- Affiliate program management
- Branded mobile app (included)
- 3rd-party integrations
- API access (Pro)
- Advanced automation
- Customer support

### Executive Forge Fit: **MEDIUM-HIGH**
- **Setup:** Higher friction - more features to configure (3-4 weeks)
- **Audience Expectations:** Same as Teachable/Circle ($50-$300/month)
- **Integration:** Excellent - everything in one platform
- **Effort:** Medium - 5-10 hours/week (all tools integrated)
- **Revenue Potential:**
  - Conservative: Courses ($30k) + community ($15k) = $45k/yr
  - Moderate: Courses ($75k) + community ($45k) + affiliates ($15k) = $135k/yr
  - Aggressive: Courses ($150k) + community ($120k) + affiliates ($50k) = $320k/yr

### Kajabi vs. Teachable vs. Circle
| Factor | Kajabi | Teachable | Circle |
|--------|--------|-----------|--------|
| All-in-one? | ✅ Yes | ❌ Course only | ❌ Community only |
| Cost | $143-399/mo | $39-399/mo | $89-199/mo |
| Courses | ✅ Strong | ✅ Excellent | ⚠️ Basic |
| Community | ✅ Strong | ❌ Basic | ✅ Excellent |
| Branding | ✅ Full control | ✅ Full control | ✅ Full control |
| Complexity | High | Medium | Medium |

### Verdict
- **Choose Kajabi if** you want everything in one dashboard (courses + community + email)
- **Choose Teachable if** your primary focus is courses
- **Choose Circle if** your primary focus is community & recurring revenue

## 7. MIGHTY NETWORKS vs. CIRCLE: Community Deep-Dive

Both are strong, but for different use cases:

### Mighty Networks
- **Best for:** High-engagement communities (challenges, peer learning, events)
- **Mobile:** Native apps (60% more engagement)
- **Cost:** $950/yr upfront
- **Revenue:** No commission, all yours
- **Engagement:** 84% member-led content (very active)
- **Ideal:** Executive Forge challenge-based community

### Circle
- **Best for:** Course + community hybrid models
- **Mobile:** Branded app option (premium feature)
- **Cost:** $89-199/month (monthly billing available)
- **Revenue:** No commission, all yours
- **Flexibility:** More customizable workflows
- **Ideal:** Executive Forge membership + courses
