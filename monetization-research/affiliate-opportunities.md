# Affiliate Opportunities for Executive Forge

## Overview
Affiliate marketing can generate $1,000-$5,000/month with minimal ongoing effort once relationships are established. Focus on tools that executive education audiences actually use.

---

## BUSINESS SOFTWARE AFFILIATES (Highest Quality Fit)

### 1. NOTION (Workspace/Wiki) ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
- **Commission:** $50 per activated signup + **20% of year 1 revenue**
- **Audience Fit:** EXCELLENT (executives use Notion for planning, wikis, databases)
- **Entry Level:** Apply at notion.partnerstack.com
- **Status:** Program currently not accepting new affiliates (check quarterly)
- **Promotion Strategy:** 
  - Create Notion templates for executive planning
  - "How I use Notion" YouTube video
  - Recommendation in email newsletter ($50 signup bonus)
- **Estimated Monthly Revenue:** $200-500/mo (with 20-50 referrals/mo at $50 + 20% rev share)
- **Effort:** Low (2-3 hours/mo for content + links)

### 2. STRIPE (Payment Processing) ⭐⭐⭐⭐
- **Commission:** Up to $2,000 per referred customer + ongoing revenue share
- **Audience Fit:** EXCELLENT (all online business models need Stripe)
- **Entry Level:** partners.stripe.com
- **Partner Tier:** Integration Partner (easiest for creators)
- **Commission Structure:** 
  - Marketplace referrals: $500-$2,000 depending on revenue
  - Ongoing: 5-10% for high-volume partners (variable)
- **Promotion Strategy:**
  - "How to set up online payments" article/video
  - Course payment setup guide
  - Recommended for anyone launching courses/products
- **Estimated Monthly Revenue:** $300-1,000/mo (fewer referrals, but higher value)
- **Effort:** Low (1-2 hours/mo)
- **Notes:** Partner program is selective; apply through partnerships contact

### 3. HUBSPOT (CRM + Marketing) ⭐⭐⭐⭐

## AFFILIATE DO's & DON'Ts

### DO
- ✅ Disclose affiliate relationships clearly (FTC requirement)
- ✅ Only promote tools you actually use
- ✅ Focus on 5-10 quality affiliates, not 50 mediocre ones
- ✅ Track which affiliates drive conversions
- ✅ Update content if you stop using a tool
- ✅ Build affiliate links into genuine recommendations

### DON'T
- ❌ Promote competitor courses on their platform
- ❌ Spam affiliate links (ruins trust)
- ❌ Promote tools that don't align with audience needs
- ❌ Forget to update commission rates annually
- ❌ Prioritize higher commissions over better tools
- ❌ Forget FTC disclosure
