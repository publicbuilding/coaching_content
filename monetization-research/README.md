# Executive Forge Monetization Research - Complete Package

## Overview

This research identifies and analyzes 5-10 new revenue streams beyond YouTube AdSense, sponsorships, and Gumroad for Executive Forge. The goal: **increase revenue from $16k/year to $100k-300k within 12 months (6-8x growth).**

### 1. **platforms-analysis.md** (15.5 KB)
Detailed analysis of 7 platforms evaluated for Executive Forge

**What's Inside:**
- Substack (paid newsletters): Free setup, 90% revenue share, $10-30k/year potential
- Maven (live cohorts): Best for live teaching, $42-210k/year potential
- Teachable (self-paced courses): Industry standard, $36-270k/year potential

- Mighty Networks (private community): Superior engagement, $5.7-189k/year potential
- Kajabi (all-in-one): Everything bundled, higher cost but complete solution
- ConvertKit (email platform): Alternative to Substack

**Key Recommendation:**
- Launch immediately: Substack + Maven
- Add by Month 4: Teachable + Circle
- Enterprise focus by Month 7: Corporate partnerships

## ⚠️ Critical Success Factors

### Must-Haves
1. **Quality content** - All platforms succeed only if course/community value is high
2. **Consistent launch** - Stick to timeline (Month 1-3 are foundational)
3. **Partnership effort** - Month 7+ is when big money happens, don't skip it
4. **Delegation** - Hire VAs by Month 4, can't scale alone

### Common Pitfalls to Avoid
- ❌ Launching all platforms at once (overwhelming)
- ❌ Focusing on low-margin revenue (affiliates only)
- ❌ Neglecting partnerships (biggest revenue potential)
- ❌ Not tracking metrics (can't improve what you don't measure)
- ❌ Giving up too early (some streams take 3-4 months to gain traction)
