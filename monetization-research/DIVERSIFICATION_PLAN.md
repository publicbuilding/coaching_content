## CONTINGENCY PLANNING

### If Maven Doesn't Scale as Expected
- **Pivot:** Allocate more effort to Teachable self-paced courses
- **Backup:** Launch group coaching as primary cohort model
- **Timeline:** Adjust by 1-2 months

### If Circle Community Underperforms
- **Pivot:** Move to Mighty Networks (better engagement)
- **Backup:** Pivot to Kajabi (courses + community in one)
- **Risk Mitigation:** Soft launch with first 50 users before heavy promotion

### If Corporate Sales Stall
- **Pivot:** Focus on association partnerships (lower ACV, faster sales)
- **Backup:** Emphasize coaching clients (higher margins, easier sales)
- **Fallback:** Increase affiliate marketing effort (passive income)

### If Sponsors Dry Up
- **Not critical:** Represents <1% of revenue by Month 12
- **Action:** Diversify from YouTube sponsors to industry sponsors

## FINAL NOTES

### Keys to Success
1. **Launch fast:** Get first 3 streams live by Month 3 (Substack, Maven, Affiliates)
2. **Focus:** Don't chase all revenue streams at once—sequence them
3. **Optimize:** Spend time on what's working, cut what isn't by Month 6
4. **Partner:** Enterprise growth comes from partnerships, not individual sales
5. **Delegate:** Hire VA by Month 4, scale team by Month 12

### Common Pitfalls to Avoid
- ❌ Trying to launch all platforms at once (overwhelming)
- ❌ Focusing on low-margin revenue (affiliates are nice, but not primary)
- ❌ Neglecting partnerships for individual course sales
- ❌ Over-engineering courses instead of shipping MVP
- ❌ Not tracking metrics (you can't improve what you don't measure)

### Confidence Intervals
This plan is **realistic but aggressive.** Factors that could accelerate growth:
- YouTube channel gains 100k+ subscribers (increases all conversion rates)
- One viral piece of content (partnership, sponsorship opportunities)
- Strong case studies from early Maven/courses customers
- One major corporate partnership (2-3x acceleration)

Factors that could slow growth:
- Market downturn reduces corporate spending
- Competition in course space
- Low community engagement (difficult to scale)

**Most likely outcome:** $1M-2M Year 1 revenue (within 6 months), scaling to $2M-5M by Year 2
